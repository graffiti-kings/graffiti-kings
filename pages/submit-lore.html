Contribute Lore

Write from the current world, with sources, consequences and clear status.

Contribute a world that still fits together

Bring a character, scene, local practice or source correction into a world that already has relationships and consequences. Real people can create and perform Crypto Moonboys characters. A new performance or draft is a contribution, not automatic central canon.

This page is a preparation guide; it does not submit material or certify acceptance. Keep the source, intended status and continuity visible in the proposal.

Start from the current story

Read the main saga, The First Witness, the source hierarchy and the master chronology. GK’s explicit current corrections govern over contradictory older site text.

An earlier W81 account, serial ending or codex remains valuable when attributed to its witness. It must not silently overwrite the public command structure or disclose a supposedly final ending that the current story leaves unresolved. The public Final Fork remains unresolved.

Useful contributions

A scene with a clear viewpoint; a local ritual with people who practise it; a culture’s virtue turning into a difficult choice; an object with a documented custodian; a source correction that distinguishes competing dates. Show who acts, why it matters and what the action costs.

Continuity boundaries

Do not recast the Bitcoin Kid Army or HODL Warriors as street factions. Do not merge HODL Warriors with HODL X Warriors. Do not make every Bitcoin X Kid an escaped soldier, every GraffPUNK a subordinate of one legend, or every realm part of a single simulation.

Do not invent a roster name to force older mixed lists into forty political slots. Avoid universal resurrection, unrestricted reality control, guaranteed victories, unsupported real biography and a newly revealed final outcome.

Name the kind of record

Label the work as a source correction, proposed fiction, character performance, local interpretation, game concept or description of a live feature. A factual claim needs a verifiable source; a fictional account needs a place in continuity; a live feature needs current implementation evidence.

Characters and performers

Keep a character’s name, allegiance, period, abilities and limits clear. Identify a performer or real creator separately where appropriate. A fictional title, age or family relationship does not establish a fact about that real person.

GRIT and Grit42 are distinct; Aleema is not made a literal child by an epithet; a Princess title does not identify every woman it names. Use the character directory and the owning source page before merging identities.

Political identity and military service

In Year 3008, crypto rules everyday life. The All City Bulls and Nomad Bears embody the market’s two sides. Both are shadowed 24/7 by undercover Bitcoin Kid Army guards. Alfie “The Bitcoin KiD” Blaze leads that Army and takes orders only from GKniftyHEADS, the underworld good guys above the two market factions and the remaining 37 GK factions.

When the underworld needs a larger army, members of all GK factions unite as the HODL Warriors while keeping their faction identities. The Bitcoin Kid Army and HODL Warriors are armies, not street factions. Every HODL Warrior aspires to become a HODL X Warrior of Block Topia, but that distinct status must be earned.

Queen Sarah P-fly rules Block Topia with her Crypto Moongirls army and the HODL X order. Inside, the engineered Bitcoin X Kids grow within the city’s systems. Some escape and join Alfie’s Army outside. Those movements connect the city and the underworld without making their forces the same.

Give a culture room to differ without assigning every office, family, subformation or temporary muster a new political seat. The commentary register includes more kinds of belonging than an exact governmental roster.

Rights, objects and rewards

Holding an NFT, performing a character and receiving a qualifying creator licence are different permissions. Describe the specific object and applicable terms. Do not infer new licences, prices, token utility or reward availability from a story.

What a reviewer needs

Supply the source passages, any conflicting witnesses, the intended place in chronology and an explicit account of what the proposal adds. Keep First Witness scripture, later commentary and a character’s opinion distinguishable.

Human review and explicit approval remain separate from drafting, indexing, an AI summary or a successful game action. This page makes no promise that every proposed addition will be accepted.

Contribution checklist

Title and type: name the piece and whether it is fiction, correction, performance or a proposed system.

People and place: name the viewpoint, relationships, realm and period, with real creators kept separate.

Source and continuity: link the owning pages, quote only what is needed, identify conflicts and explain the proposed resolution.

Action and consequence: show the choice, cost, limits and what remains unresolved.

Status and rights: state proposed or approved status and any applicable licence or implementation evidence.
