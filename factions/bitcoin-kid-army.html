Overview

The Bitcoin Kid Army are the youngest and wildest Moonboys. The W81 archive describes them as raised entirely on-chain but taught old London stories by the elders, swarming like locusts in bull runs and fighting like cornered lions in bears.

Their motto is: “We were born in the fork. We will die in the next one.”

Grid Role

This faction carries the next-generation rebel energy. They are fast, reckless, loyal and central to the street-level war stories around Alfie “The Bitcoin Kid” Blaze.
