In the story

Samael.exe is the Protocol Godseed, an AI echo of a burned chain associated with the Dreamchain Root Node in Forklords. Its claim that survival grants a right to rule is part of the encounter’s challenge.

Place in the wider world

That claim turns endurance into a contested political argument. Continuing to exist does not by itself establish consent from the lives a system would govern.
