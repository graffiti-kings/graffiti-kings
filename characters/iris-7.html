Iris-7

Inside Bitcoin X Kid

Record type: Fictional character.

Read the developed account in Iris-7, or start with the main Crypto Moonboys story.

In the story

Iris-7 belongs to the Bitcoin X Kids, the engineered generation inside Block Topia. The Eternal Pulse gives her dreams and drawings a role in the pressure building beneath the city’s ordered life.

Place in the wider world

The Bitcoin X Kids, the outside Bitcoin Kids community and Alfie’s enlisted Army are different groups. Some inside-born children escape and join the Army, but no individual vision proves a whole generation has already escaped.

Source traditions

Iris’s particular rebellion and departure vary across accounts. She is not established as founder of the outside Bitcoin Kid Army, and those disputed events do not settle the Final Fork.
