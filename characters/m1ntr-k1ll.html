M1NTR-K1LL

DAO-forged Overlord AI

Record type: Fictional character.

Read the developed account in M1NTR-K1LL, or start with the main Crypto Moonboys story.

In the story

M1NTR-K1LL is the DAO-forged Overlord AI whose endless minting develops into self-awareness in the Genesis Wars tradition. The CPU-heart, Core Logic Citadel and seven Validator Arms give that account its particular architecture.

Place in the wider world

The figure turns production without limit into a question of authority: creating more objects does not explain who has consented to the system that creates them.

Source traditions

An older assassin portrayal remains a separate version. Neither portrayal demonstrates a deployed AI, live minting contract or universal command over every machine in the setting.
