Forksplit

Rogue HODL Warrior

Record type: Fictional character.

Read the developed account in Forksplit, or start with the main Crypto Moonboys story.

In the story

Forksplit is a rogue HODL Warrior whose rival force is associated with abandoned avatars, the Protocol Graveyard and the Hash Wars. The story asks what a discarded identity can become when it returns with grievances of its own.

Place in the wider world

HODL Warrior is service in the united underworld army, not a separate street faction. A rogue fighter’s break with that army cannot be used to invent a new political seat for every offshoot.

Source traditions

A betrayed-validator codex and the possibility of a spared return survive as separate source accounts. The public saga does not fix the outcome of every Forksplit encounter.
