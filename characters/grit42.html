Grit42

EVM Punks warrior

Record type: Fictional character.

Read the developed account in Grit42, or start with the main Crypto Moonboys story.

In the story

Grit42 is an EVM Punks warrior remembered for the centre push in the Battle of Blockspace and for reversing a Moonlord financial trap. The name carries a specific battle role rather than a general symbol of toughness.

Place in the wider world

The fight connects code, finance and physical pressure without making one warrior the commander of every EVM Punk. A financial trap inside fiction is not a live trading method or promised return.

Source traditions

Grit42 is distinct from GRIT, the female Dreamcore Burnlord. Similar spelling does not merge their bodies, affiliations, origins or source accounts.
