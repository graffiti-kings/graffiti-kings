Charlie Buster

Grime Art · NO BALL GAMES · Real artist and connected projects.

Record type: Real creator.

Artist, brand and documented projects

Charlie Buster is a real artist connected to Graffiti Kings, Grime Art and NO BALL GAMES. His public work and projects are documented through this site’s separate source-led records, rather than established by a fictional job title.

The earlier website’s “wiki builder” and “archive operator” descriptions were interpretive roles. They are not evidence of employment, authorship of every wiki page or ownership of the entire Crypto Moonboys world.

Follow the work

Grime Art and the original-work record connects artwork listings and public accounts. The source directory distinguishes artist statements, collector listings, press and social channels.

NO BALL GAMES, $NBG and XRP KIDs have their own project history. TREEF is recorded as a published proposal rather than a completed engineering or environmental result.

The character role is a separate layer

Real creators can make and perform Crypto Moonboys characters. The NoBallGames Legion’s fictional Pitch Lords tradition draws on a real visual brand without turning the artist’s biography into a military service record.

Legion members can serve in the united HODL Warriors with members of all GK factions. The combined army is not a new faction, and a fictional fan-token empire does not prove real adoption, rewards or live financial terms.

Keep the attribution clear

Use a dated public source for career claims and the owning lore account for a fictional event. Character powers, future dates, titles and family stories do not supply real personal facts. Creator rights also depend on the relevant terms, not a role assigned by an earlier site summary.
