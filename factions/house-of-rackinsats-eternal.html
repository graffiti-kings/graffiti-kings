House of Rackinsats

Fictional household and mutual-aid tradition

Record type: Fictional household and mutual-aid tradition.

Read the developed account in House of Rackinsats, or start with the main Crypto Moonboys story.

Identity and role

The House of Rackinsats begins as an underground family of organisers, painters, mechanics and cooks. Its remembered warmth is material: a workshop, food, borrowed power and someone willing to stand beside a neighbour facing eviction.

The W13 family account places Papa Des in South Croydon’s early-1980s red-brick terraces, raising Denise and Tracey after their mother’s death. These are fictional family records, not proof of contemporary people’s birth dates or private relationships.

Life and practice

Agent SAM turns household obligations into an archive. The raid that takes Denise, Tracey and Forge turns that archive toward war. The porch is a place where responsibility is practised before it becomes a grand theory.

The household’s obligations to make the streets speak, preserve trust and protect joy are inherited rules. They do not add new Books to the common First Witness or command every other culture.

Command and relationships

Porch Poets, the Eternal Porch Keepers and Rackinsats Fashion House have related older archive traditions. A shared household image does not prove that every office and service unit holds an independent seat in the Forty.

The modern command structure remains GKniftyHEADS above the two market factions and the remaining 37 GK factions, with all-faction service in the HODL Warriors when needed.

Sources and boundaries

The developed household record distinguishes family story, divine-lineage theology and conflicting calendars. Preserve those different witnesses rather than resetting characters’ ages or presenting theology as real biography.
