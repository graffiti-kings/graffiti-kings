NULL THE PROPHET

Antichain threat · Year 3008

Record type: Fictional character.

Read the developed account in NULL THE PROPHET, or start with the main Crypto Moonboys story.

In the story

NULL THE PROPHET names the Antichain threat: a force that breaks the relationship between records and the lives they should preserve. A name can remain legible while losing the person it names; a route can survive on a map while losing its destination. This is more than ordinary censorship or a missing archive file.

Accounts disagree on whether NULL is a being, a signal, a corruption, a cult’s revelation or several phenomena gathered under one name. The danger is firmer than its origin: witnesses can lose the terms in which testimony and disagreement remain possible.

Place in the wider world

The Bulls, Bears, Bitcoin Kid Army, united HODL Warriors and Block Topia’s forces confront this threat while still carrying conflicts of their own. Keeping territory or winning an arena cannot alone defeat a danger that attacks the meaning of allegiance and victory.

Chain Scribes, artists, couriers and ordinary witnesses matter alongside fighters because the defence concerns the connections between people and their records. Preserving a record must not turn its living subject into property.

Source traditions

Dramatic transmissions give NULL a voice, bargains and cruelty; other dossiers describe recursive contradictions in memory cores. These are distinct narrative witnesses, not proof of one settled body or continuous personality. NULL is a central antagonist threat, superseding this site’s earlier description of a faction-neutral warning figure. The Final Fork remains unresolved.
