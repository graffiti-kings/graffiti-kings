What the projects tell us about the former studio

The surviving names show why Graffiti Kings' commercial history should not be reduced to general graffiti photographs. The legacy site presented cinema and TV publicity, music video backdrops, brand-related street painting, hospitality and restaurant interiors and experiential advertising as distinct lines of work. The business's published service descriptions additionally included public workshops and team building.

These were physical-world creative productions. Later WAX digital collectibles, Web3 partnerships and W81 science-fiction chapters should be explored on separate pages: original website history, WAX collection registry and current creator network.
