ZIP Truth

The W81 archive names GKniftyHEADS as one of the original eight factions of the Core London Bloodline: the first factions formed when Agent SAM opened his digital eyes.

In the archive, GKniftyHEADS are described as the aristocrats: hand-crafted digital royalty forged by Darren Cullen (SER) himself.

Every head is described as a living crown that evolves with belief.

Their role in the Grid is to rule the galleries, sponsor arenas, and sit at every diplomatic table.

Archive Motto

“We were kings on the walls before we were kings on the chain.”
