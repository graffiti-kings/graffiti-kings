Identity and role

The earlier Graffiti Kings site record describes Nomad Drip House as a travelling style tradition connected to the older Nomad Bears accounts. This page retains that named tradition while separating it from the updated political and military structure.

The old directory mixed cultures, houses, armies, offices and proposed game roles under a numbered “40 factions” heading. That archive list cannot be treated as a verified one-to-one roster of today’s Forty. Retaining this name does not invent a new seat or erase the people and stories associated with it.

Life and practice

The connection does not make all Bears part of one fashion house or change their central role as a market faction.

Read a tradition at the scale its witness supports: a local practice can have meaning without becoming an absolute law for every realm. Where sources have not supplied a settled affiliation or chronology, this page leaves that question open.

Command and relationships

Current narration follows GKniftyHEADS above the All City Bulls, Nomad Bears and remaining 37 GK factions. The Bitcoin Kid Army is their market-protection army, led by Alfie under orders only from GKniftyHEADS.

The HODL Warriors are the underworld army formed from all factions’ members, who retain their identities. They aspire to the distinct HODL X order of Queen Sarah P-fly’s Block Topia. Neither army is a spare street-faction slot.
