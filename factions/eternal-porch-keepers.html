ETERNAL PORCH KEEPERS

Faction 24 — Maintainers of the House of Rackinsats recreation

Overview

The Eternal Porch Keepers are the final faction of the Economic and Belief Guardians group. In the W81 faction structure, they maintain the House of Rackinsats recreation so the universe never forgets the human house at the centre of the myth. Their work is not loud. They do not exist to dominate arenas, chase clout, or overwrite walls. They exist to keep the family memory intact while the Grid expands into games, chains, factions, wars and future-history.

Where other factions guard tokens, tags, routes, prophecies or arenas, the Eternal Porch Keepers guard the domestic root: the porch, the family names, the remembered voices, the normal human setting that stops the universe becoming empty cyber-fantasy. They are a memory faction, a boundary faction and a stabilising faction. Their value is measured by what does not get lost.

Canon Role

Their canon role is preservation. They keep the House of Rackinsats recreation active inside the Grid and treat the Eternal Porch as more than scenery. It is a sanctuary, a reset point, a place where conflicting factions are reminded that the archive began with people before it became a chain-world. The Keepers maintain names, rituals, family references, porch stories, inherited phrases and the soft social codes that the harder factions depend on without always admitting it.

When the Sky Spire, HODL Layer, arena factions and rebel factions become too detached from the original root, the Eternal Porch Keepers pull the story back to the house. They make the myth smaller on purpose so it can remain true.

Culture and Visual Language

The Eternal Porch Keepers carry a warmer, older visual language than most Grid factions. Their symbols are porches, railings, chairs, doorways, old photographs, hand-painted signs, soft yellow light, memory tags and archived household objects. Their marks are not only graffiti marks; they are acts of care. A Keeper tag might appear beside a family quote, a threshold, a protected wall or a restored fragment of South London memory.

They are not passive. In W81 terms, remembering is an active defence. A system that forgets its root becomes easy to rewrite. The Keepers prevent that rewrite.

Canon Boundaries

This page should stay aligned with W81. The Eternal Porch Keepers should not be rewritten as generic monks, fantasy priests or soft background characters. Their canon power is specific: they maintain the Eternal Porch and protect the human family memory at the centre of the Graffiti Kings / Crypto Moonboys archive.
