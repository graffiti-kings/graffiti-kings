Jodie ZOOM 2000

GraffPUNK-born seer · Forkborn refusal

Record type: Fictional character.

Read the developed account in Jodie ZOOM 2000, or start with the main Crypto Moonboys story.

In the story

Jodie is a GraffPUNK-born seer and Hard Fork Games survivor whose painted dream-glyphs and timeline doors make memory a contested source of power. She rejects an offer of belonging that requires the erasure of her past.

She escapes Block Topia with Aleema and a third rejected recruit whose name is not preserved. Their refusal is foundational to the Forkborn, associated with the Shard Mothers of Manhattan. Jodie leads within that tradition without becoming ruler of every GraffPUNK.

Place in the wider world

Her mother’s resistance paintings on power-plant walls explain why public marks matter to her. A wall can preserve testimony that an official record excludes. Her evidence about the treatment of female emissaries challenges the city’s promises of perfect order.

Echo Ink, doors and divided timelines belong to particular stories with consequences. They are not a licence to erase any loss, repair every contradiction or move every character without cost.

Source traditions

Accounts place the Shard Mothers association at different points around the escape. The connection is established; a precise universal founding date is not. “Echo Mother” is a title used by a rebellious synthetic Moongirl splinter, not a claim that Jodie is biological mother or owner of every Moongirl.
