Overview

Iris-7 is the eye motif made into a character: a watcher figure inside the Graffiti Kings / GKniftyHEADS archive. The W81 universe repeatedly uses systems of memory, scanning, hidden signals, prophecy, faction movement and corrupted timelines. Iris-7 belongs to that family of characters who do not simply act inside the world but observe the world until its patterns become readable.

She should not be reduced to a generic robot or spy. Iris-7 is more useful as a surveillance-poet of the Grid: a figure who sees tags before they are buffed, tracks wallet trails before they are understood, and notices when a character, faction or page has been altered by a fork.

Watcher Function

The watcher role is different from a ruler or soldier. Iris-7 does not need to command armies to matter. She gives the archive its nervous system. Every wiki needs a way to notice contradictions, dead links, overwritten memories and fake canon. In lore terms, Iris-7 is one of the figures who can detect those breaks.

Her strongest function is pattern recognition. She reads the city as a layered surface: wall paint, old tags, metadata, transaction movement, faction allegiance and hidden signals all become part of one field of vision. That makes her valuable to prophets, scribes, players and holders who need to understand what has changed.

Grid Presence

Inside the Grid, Iris-7 can be used as a bridge between surveillance and protection. She watches not because the world needs control, but because the archive can be attacked through forgetting. A fake page, a corrupted origin, a bad claim or an unsourced timeline can damage the universe just as much as an enemy faction.

This gives Iris-7 a clear future wiki role: she can appear in pages about canon review, archive protection, faction monitoring, timeline repair and hidden-message discovery. She is a natural companion to pages about SAM, GK Brain, NULL THE PROPHET and Elder Codex-7, but she should remain her own identity.

Visual Language

Iris-7 should visually centre the eye, lens, scanline, camera, optic tag and grid overlay. The design should feel street-tech rather than sterile science fiction. A clean eye symbol over a rough wall, a visor full of tag fragments, or a character marked with surveillance glyphs would fit the wiki better than generic cyberpunk detail.

Her page should encourage future art to stay clear and iconic. She should be readable as an eye/watcher even at small scale. Any complex detail should support that silhouette rather than bury it.

Canon Boundary

This profile is a W81-aligned foundation page. It does not define an exact faction allegiance, confirmed species, precise age, fixed power level or definitive origin event unless later source material supports it. Iris-7's canon strength is her function: watching, reading, detecting and protecting the archive.

Future additions should be evidence-led. Useful expansions include recovered W81 excerpts, approved story fragments, image notes, NFT metadata links and documented appearances. Unsupported spy-thriller detail, random villain arcs and fake mission history should be removed.
