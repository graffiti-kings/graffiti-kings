THE COMPLETE 40 FACTIONS

Every tribe, bloodline, role and rivalry in the Eternal Grid

What the Factions Are

The W81 archive defines the Crypto Moonboys faction system as a forty-part split of the same living source. The factions are not decorative teams, loose fan labels or shallow categories. They are forty different expressions of one cultural memory: the South London street root, the Graffiti Kings lineage, the GKniftyHEADS crown layer, the GraffPUNKS rebellion, the HODLWARRIORS belief layer, the NoBallGames arena culture, the Crypto Moongirls optimisation layer and the Eternal Porch family memory that holds the whole structure together.

The faction bible begins from the Triple Fork: the event that split the soul of Block Topia into living tribes. Each tribe carries a specific job inside the Grid. Some paint. Some guard. Some build arenas. Some maintain lore. Some travel across chains. Some protect the family root. Some watch for the next Hard Fork. The point of the system is not that every faction stands alone; the point is that every faction is one beat of the same drum.

This directory is now the live hub for all forty W81 factions. Every entry links to a long-form faction page. The pages use the archive as truth, preserve the W81 faction grouping, and avoid adding unsupported outside lore.

Section II — The Creative Rebels

The Creative Rebels weaponise beauty, paint, pattern, poetry and narrated memory. They defend the wall-born soul of the Grid from being flattened into clean corporate architecture.

Burn Crews

Elite 40-man fireteams deploying self-replicating NFT tags faster than scrub systems can erase them.

Stencil Witches

Female GraffPUNKS turning sacred South Croydon patterns into reality-bending sigils.

Tag Lords

Apex predators of the paint layer, evolving Darren’s throw-up style into data weaponry.

Brush Daughters

Marcus “Brush” Kane lineage whose strokes become permanent on-chain law.

Echo Mothers

Narrators and archivists connected to Jodie Zoom 2000’s elite order.

Wildstyle Collective

Chaotic-good artists who reject governance and paint what the Grid needs in the moment.

Fractal Taggers

Moongirl defectors blending Sarah P-fly elegance with raw street chaos.

Porch Poets

Papa Des’s spoken-word heirs, encoding poetry into crisis-triggered smart contracts.

Section III — Economic & Belief Guardians

The Economic and Belief Guardians were born from the Whale Lords War Arc. They control conviction, reserve strength, immovable holding power and the diplomatic structure that stops the Sky Spire and HODL Layer from tearing apart.

Deep Holders

The original 198 wallets multiplied into the immovable foundation of the belief economy.

Whale Lords Eternal

Awakened OGs whose bags are older and heavier than the algorithms.

HODL Layer Sentinels

Elite guards patrolling the border between the Optimisation Layer and the HODL Layer.

Compound Believers

Long-term staking ritualists who reshape local physics around their wallets.

Genesis Anchors

Wallets that have never transacted, treated as the literal reserve of the economy.

Porch Accord Diplomats

Negotiators keeping the Sky Spire and HODL Layer from fully separating.

Belief Miners

They mine conviction from new holders and convert it into on-chain power.

Eternal Porch Keepers

Maintainers of the recreated family root so the London source is never erased.

Section IV — Game & Arena Masters

The Game and Arena Masters are the playable layer of the W81 world. They turn faction memory into matches, quests, scoreboards, bins, tag-runs, keeper leagues and arcade records.

BinLords

Masters of landing zones, bins, borough-coded arenas and street-level scoring moments.

Tag Rush Crew

Speed-tag competitors built around momentum, reflex, paint pressure and timed runs.

League of Keepers

The rules-and-rankings order guarding fair play, tournaments and the integrity of arena culture.

Arcade Scribes

Score recorders, scoreboard keepers and lore clerks of the playable arcade layer.

Quest Givers

Mission authors converting faction memory into tasks, prompts and playable journeys.

Pixel Pushers

Sprite-builders and pixel technicians making the Grid readable through game art.

Level Lords

World builders, difficulty shapers and stage rulers of the faction game architecture.

Canon Rules

This faction directory is governed by the W81 archive. A faction page may expand tone, structure and readable explanation, but it cannot invent unsupported faction powers, leaders, origins or timelines outside the archive. When a faction is unclear, the page must preserve that uncertainty instead of pretending the archive is more complete than it is.

The correct standard is full wiki depth, not short stubs. A proper faction page should explain origin, function, cultural role, visual language, allies, tensions, system position and canon boundaries while staying tied to the W81 faction bible.
