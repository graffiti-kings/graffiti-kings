HODL X Warriors

Block Topia champion and specialist-service order

Record type: Block Topia champion and specialist-service order.

Read the developed account in HODL X Warriors, or start with the main Crypto Moonboys story.

Identity and role

The HODL X Warriors belong to Block Topia under Queen Sarah P-fly and her Crypto Moongirls army. They are the city’s champion and specialist-service order, with selection, earned status and obligations after survival.

They are distinct from the HODL Warriors, the combined underworld army drawn from every GK faction. Every HODL Warrior aspires to become a HODL X Warrior, but wanting the title does not grant it.

Life and practice

Hard Fork selection, bonnets, armour modification and repair, Seeding accounts and service obligations give the champion tradition its material life. Status changes what a person may access and what the city may demand in return.

A champion can carry obligations that predate a local underworld mobilisation. Working beside a HODL Warrior at a crossing does not prove that both have always belonged to the same order.

Command and relationships

The city’s engineered Bitcoin X Kids can travel in the opposite direction: some escape and join Alfie’s Bitcoin Kid Army outside. Those movements connect the worlds without making either pair of names synonymous.

Queen Sarah’s city and the underworld both face NULL. Their common enemy does not erase disagreements about consent, identity, selection or the Queen’s control.

Sources and boundaries

Champion and Seeding traditions are fictional accounts, not a grant of present-day creator rights or a live reward entitlement. The Games and the developed order record preserve local differences. The Final Fork remains unresolved.
