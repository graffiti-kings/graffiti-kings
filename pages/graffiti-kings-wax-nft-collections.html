GRAFFITI KINGS: WAX NFT COLLECTIONS

A source-led register of named legacy collection accounts and the project’s documented WAX history, separated from speculative market and future-feature claims.

Scope: collection names rather than invented supply numbers

The Graffiti Kings / GKniftyHEADS digital project has used the WAX blockchain for NFT artworks and game-related collectibles. The project's own GraffPUNKS NFT guide names six legacy collections and a seventh collection called gkniftyheads. This page preserves the spelling of those accounts and provides direct marketplace links without inventing sales, wallet counts or asset balances.

Marketplaces display collection metadata submitted or controlled by collection accounts; a public listing is evidence that a marketplace recognises the identifier, not proof of particular artists’ identity, ownership of a web domain or currently available utility. Third-party market statistics may change or fail to load.

What the project claims and what is independently documented

The Blockleaders profile dated 31 August 2022 provides a contemporary independent report about Darren Cullen, Graffiti Kings and experiments with WAX NFTs, games and the LFGK token. It is a useful dated bridge between the older street-art practice and the blockchain era.

The GraffPUNKS collection guide claims that giveaways began in May 2020, describes a large recipient community and discusses a plan to burn assets from its original six collections. Another project-authored Substack article asserts substantial historic trading volume and burned supply. These totals are promotional claims, not independently reconciled blockchain measurements. No current market capitalisation, floor price, market volume or collectible value is represented as verified by this archive.

The account names in the table above are more durable identifiers than screenshots of current sale prices, which can quickly become inaccurate. Projects may also change mint permissions and transfer policies after an article is published.

NFT types, gaming and announced utility

GraffPUNKS describes three categories: older Graffiti Kings NFTs, proposed GK.$MArT interactive gaming assets and NFT promotions from collaborators. Its guide mentions 1,600 proposed GK.$MArT HODL WARRIORS linked to forty factions, plus reward mechanisms involving coupons, burns and gaming. These descriptions document a project plan or promotion; they do not establish that every asset or entitlement has been delivered.

Research on historical NFTs should not automatically become financial advice. Holding a collectible does not guarantee payouts, playable utility, positive resale value or continued project support. Verify the smart contracts and current rules before relying on any rewards described in older publicity.

How to verify instead of repeating marketing totals

For any of the seven identifiers, open the linked NFT Hive collection page and examine its current templates, assets, holders and market activity. Check the collection account and its contract on the WAX chain using an independent block explorer or AtomicAssets-compatible chain API before reporting minted totals, burned totals, supply or sale history. Collection marketing text alone is insufficient.

Confirm whether the collection is still mintable using actual contract-authority records rather than relying on an older statement that minting has stopped. Do not presume a link to a marketplace is proof that an NFT is still listed for sale. Live prices are deliberately excluded.

Decentraland and Graffiti Queens: related history, different records

The Graffiti Queens 2021 exhibition took place in Decentraland and brought artists minting on different platforms into a shared virtual exhibition. It is an exhibition history record, not one of the seven WAX collection accounts. Likewise, Decentraland's 2021 Art Week closing event was a separate programme from the April Graffiti Queens show.

SYNC, Alien Worlds and game-related NFTs

A 2022 Alien Worlds Galactic Hubs grant-recipient notice identifies Darren Cullen and Ian Harrison as Development grantees for the proposed HODL Warriors game, which included plans for 10,000 Alien Worlds-inspired battle-deck NFTs. The grant announcement is verifiable independently of Graffiti Kings promotion, but does not establish a final minted quantity. Earlier SYNC Network posts named Graffiti Kings in a separate 2021 NFT/Decentraland partnership. Read the source-led partnerships timeline before interpreting historic game announcements as current features.

Bitcoin Kid toys and today's creator-rights distinction

Invasion Toys' Bitcoin Kid collaboration describes proposed physical vinyl rewards and 10,000 WAX NFTs in historic announcements, but neither page is an on-chain audit of final minting. The maintained GKniftyHEADS wiki describes qualifying unique 1/1 licences separately from ordinary NFT editions, while the current Crypto Moonboys guide distinguishes the free creator routes from optional ownership. These are different timelines and ownership systems; do not infer that older NFT reward promotions confer today's creator licences.

How WAXP supports these collections

Every collection above belongs to the WAX NFT ecosystem; WAXP is the native WAX network coin, not a collection or a permission to mint. Collectors may encounter WAXP as the settlement currency and resource-access asset when transferring, listing and buying NFTs. A transaction can also require CPU, NET and RAM; marketplace availability and prices are not verified from a collection name alone. The comprehensive WAXP & Graffiti Kings guide explains funding, wallet safety, AtomicAssets provenance, staking, governance, project-issued tokens and collection verification.
