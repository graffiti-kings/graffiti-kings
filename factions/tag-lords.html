Tag Lords

GraffPUNK subformation — not an extra political seat

Record type: GraffPUNK subformation — not an extra political seat.

Read the developed account in Tag Lords, or start with the main Crypto Moonboys story.

Identity and role

Tag Lords are high-status GraffPUNK tag operators whose older accounts connect inherited throw-up style to data warfare. This is a subformation within the GraffPUNK archive tradition, rather than an automatic independent street faction added to the Forty.

The earlier website numbered these names alongside armies and houses. That numbering recorded a mixed archive list; it is not the current political roster.

Life and practice

The tradition gives stories a specific concern: signature, territorial reputation and the danger of treating recognition as ownership. Retaining that practice is more useful than granting a generic limitless ability to every member.

Self-replicating tags, reality-bending sigils and data weapons belong to particular fictional accounts. They do not guarantee permanent erasure immunity, universal control or a live technical feature.

Command and relationships

The wider GraffPUNKS culture has many makers and operators. No subformation is established here as owner of the whole Paint Path.

Members can serve in the combined HODL Warriors underworld army without losing their GraffPUNK identity. That army and Block Topia’s HODL X order remain distinct.
