Nomad Bears

One of the two main market factions

Record type: One of the two main market factions.

Read the developed account in Nomad Bears, or start with the main Crypto Moonboys story.

Identity and role

The Nomad Bears are the other main Crypto Moonboys faction beside the All City Bulls. Together they embody the bears and bulls of the market. GK’s updated brief also uses “Nomad Bares”; this site retains the established Bears title and URL.

They are wanderers, observers, artists and couriers on ruined routes through the Street Kingdoms. Solitude is an important freedom, but a traveller still needs repairs, warmth, refuge and somebody who notices an overdue return.

Life and practice

The Bears organise care for routes and damaged places without insisting that everyone must live beneath one permanent roof. Their First Witness reading centres the Fork and the Keeper: freedom to separate, and responsibility for what one carries.

Independence can become isolation. Refusing a ruler can become refusing an inconvenient neighbour. Their stories turn on that tension rather than presenting every Bear as an untouchable loner.

Command and relationships

The Bitcoin Kid Army keeps undercover guards close to every Bear and Bull 24/7. Alfie commands the Army and takes orders only from GKniftyHEADS.

Bears join the HODL Warriors when members of all GK factions muster for a larger underworld force. Their routes and disagreements remain part of their culture inside that common army.

Sources and boundaries

Claims that Bears are immune to NULL are uncertain traditions and a reason some become targets. They are not a guaranteed species-wide immunity. Earlier chain-hopping imagery can describe their travelling culture without replacing their present market-faction role.
