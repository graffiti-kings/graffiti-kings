Fractal Taggers

Named culture in the living First Witness register

Record type: Named culture in the living First Witness register.

Read the developed account in Fractal Taggers, or start with the main Crypto Moonboys story.

Identity and role

Fractal Taggers are a culture joining disciplined geometry to the pressure of street improvisation. The name survives in the living-world faction commentaries as well as the earlier website roster. It should keep its own cultural life rather than disappear because older lists disagree.

A culture’s membership, an office held within it and temporary army service are different relationships. The current political structure is GKniftyHEADS, the two market factions and the remaining 37 GK factions; the commentary register includes other kinds of traditions and is not a one-to-one seat list.

Life and practice

Their practice values changing a repeated pattern when people need a different route. Its danger is turning a beautiful pattern into another rule nobody can question. A First Witness reading should allow both the virtue and the harm it can become.

Local teaching, ritual and inherited memory can differ without creating a separate Covenant for every culture. The common First Witness retains its nine Books; later commentary is interpretation.

Command and relationships

Members can take part in the united HODL Warriors while retaining their culture and faction identity. This does not turn that army into another street faction.

Block Topia’s controlled order, the outside market factions and the threat of NULL remain part of the larger world. No local artistic tradition supplies an automatic final victory or unrestricted power over reality.

Sources and boundaries

Read Faction Commentaries, The Forty Paths and ritual variations together. Earlier Moongirl-defector, Papa Des and style-lineage descriptions remain local origins where a particular witness supports them, not new universal rules.
