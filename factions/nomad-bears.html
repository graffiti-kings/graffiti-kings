🐻 Nomad Bears

The Chain-Hop Wanderers — Core London Bloodline

🐻
SectionCore London Bloodline
Archive RoleThe Chain-Hop Wanderers

ZIP Truth

The W81 archive names Nomad Bears as one of the original eight Core London Bloodline factions.

They are described as the travellers.

They move between every layer and every new blockchain.

They carry the Moonboy flag and plant seeds of the London story wherever they go.

The archive states they are the reason the universe feels infinite.
