Thera-9

Moongirl defector · Punk Net witness

Record type: Fictional character.

Read the developed account in Thera-9, or start with the main Crypto Moonboys story.

In the story

Thera-9 is a former Crypto Moongirl whose allegations about Block Topia’s neural-copy protocols connect the city’s dissidents with GraffPUNK resistance. A detailed Hard Fork account dates her flight to 2994 and places her meeting with scouts in the Bronx wastelands.

Her knowledge comes with responsibility. She has left the institution that trained her, but leaving does not erase everything she once helped administer. Sharing evidence must not turn the people within that evidence into assets again.

Place in the wider world

GraffPUNK accounts call her a founder of the clandestine Punk Net. The network links people with incompatible interests and does not become her private property or permanent army.

Later serials connect her with Jodie, Aleema and the Forkborn. These relationships put her inside the struggle over consent, record keeping and the Queen’s order, rather than making her merely a generic rogue synthetic figure.

Source traditions

The 2994 flight is attached to one detailed account, not a complete calendar for her whole life. Later completed-war outcomes and total claims about the Queen remain disputed. Thera’s testimony is consequential evidence, not proof that every allegation has already received a final verdict.
