About Graffiti Kings and Crypto Moonboys

The real movement, the creator brand and the fictional cast.

What this wiki connects

Graffiti Kings is the real street-art root of a connected creator ecosystem. This wiki documents the movement’s public record and the fictional worlds that grow from it. A documented mural, an NFT account, a proposed game and a Year 3008 battle are connected subjects with different kinds of evidence.

Crypto Moonboys is both a brand and a cast of characters real people can create and perform. The central lore entrance carries the full story; this site supplies biographies, source routes and clear relationships.

Graffiti Kings: the street root

Walls, tags, public art, crews and remembered authorship give the mythology its language. Real-world claims belong beside published dates and attributable records, not beside a fictional power used as proof of a career.

Read Darren Cullen / SER, the documentary timeline, the original-site archive and historic work pages for that paper trail.

GKniftyHEADS: identity and underworld authority

In Year 3008, crypto rules everyday life. The All City Bulls and Nomad Bears embody the market’s two sides. Both are shadowed 24/7 by undercover Bitcoin Kid Army guards. Alfie “The Bitcoin KiD” Blaze leads that Army and takes orders only from GKniftyHEADS, the underworld good guys above the two market factions and the remaining 37 GK factions.

In the creator ecosystem, GKniftyHEADS is an identity foundation with an associated art and collection history. In fiction, the Immortal Architects preserve fragmented memory and lead the underworld structure. Neither use grants blanket rights to every connected work.

GraffPUNKS: culture, stories and broadcast

The fictional GraffPUNKS are a plural street culture of painters, scouts, cipherkeepers and rebels. Burn Crews, Stencil Witches and Tag Lords are older GraffPUNK subformations, not automatically extra political seats.

The real GraffPUNKS radio and publishing network gives the culture a broadcast and written archive. A published story is evidence of its own account; it does not make every fictional attack a historical event.

Crypto Moonboys: brand and cast

Crypto Moonboys is a creator brand and a fantasy-world cast. Real people can make, inhabit and perform characters, as performers bring a film’s cast to life. The story grows from Graffiti Kings’ street-art roots into many realms, divided records and a struggle over who gets to own a name, a memory or a future.

When the underworld needs a larger army, members of all GK factions unite as the HODL Warriors while keeping their faction identities. The Bitcoin Kid Army and HODL Warriors are armies, not street factions. Every HODL Warrior aspires to become a HODL X Warrior of Block Topia, but that distinct status must be earned.

Block Topia and the wider realms

Queen Sarah P-fly rules Block Topia with her Crypto Moongirls army and the HODL X order. Inside, the engineered Bitcoin X Kids grow within the city’s systems. Some escape and join Alfie’s Army outside. Those movements connect the city and the underworld without making their forces the same.

All face NULL THE PROPHET, the Antichain threat that can sever a record from the life it should preserve. Other wars, rivalries and obligations continue. A common enemy does not settle the struggle between the street and the Queen, and the public story leaves the Final Fork unresolved.

The GK Grid includes cultural and tactical coordination. Block Topia’s True Bitcoin Fork is a distinct governing system. The World Chain, Sacred / Aether-Chain, dream realms and forked timelines must not be collapsed into one universal machine.

Archives, SAM and interpretation

SAM is a fictional intelligence and memory figure as well as a name appearing in project tool traditions. A story about SAM is not proof of a deployed assistant or a system’s current capabilities.

The wiki links witnesses and identifies conflicts. It does not turn retrieval, an AI-generated summary or an old list number into automatic canon approval. See The Long Transmission for how teaching survives censorship and reinterpretation.
