All City Bulls

One of the two main market factions

Record type: One of the two main market factions.

Read the developed account in All City Bulls, or start with the main Crypto Moonboys story.

Identity and role

The All City Bulls, also styled AllCity Bulls, are one of the two main Crypto Moonboys factions. Together with the Nomad Bears they embody the bulls and bears of the market in a world where crypto rules everyday life.

Their Year 3008 culture values achievement that others can see. Arena crews, competitors, market operators and supporters gather around public contests, champions and claims tied to performance. AllCity is an ambition to be recognised everywhere, not proof that they govern every district.

Life and practice

Contests can make common rules visible after the world’s fracture. They can also reduce every difference between people to a ranking someone must lose. The Bulls’ strength is sustained public presence; their danger is confusing recognition with entitlement to dominate.

The First Witness reading places their attention on the Wall and the Hold. Records of effort matter because an achievement can disappear from public memory while its cost remains with the person who earned it.

Command and relationships

The Bitcoin Kid Army shadows Bulls and Bears 24/7 as undercover guards. Alfie leads that force under orders only from GKniftyHEADS. Neither market faction privately owns the Army.

Bulls can join members of all GK factions in the united HODL Warriors without surrendering their faction identity. Every HODL Warrior aspires to the distinct HODL X status in Block Topia; the aspiration is not automatic admission.

Sources and boundaries

The developed Bulls account and faction commentaries give the fuller culture. Competition is fictional social practice, not a promise about real asset prices or guaranteed success.
