Record type: Fictional character.

In the story

The Whitewasher is Purifier Drone V69, a Nullsong-corrupted sanitation machine in the Under-Brooklyn Maintenance Depths tradition. A system built to clean becomes a concrete threat to the marks and people its city should preserve.

Place in the wider world

The encounter gives erasure a machine and a location. It does not establish that all sanitation, all blank walls or every act of forgetting follows one controller.

Source traditions

Spectral eraser and informant tales survive separately. They should not be collapsed into one definitive body or used to guarantee any later outcome against NULL.
