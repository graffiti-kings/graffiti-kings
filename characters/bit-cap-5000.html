Bit-Cap 5000

GraffPUNK legend and augmented fighter

Record type: Fictional character.

Read the developed account in Bit-Cap 5000, or start with the main Crypto Moonboys story.

In the story

Bit-Cap 5000 is a named GraffPUNK legend within the augmented-fighter tradition. His place is among contested street stories of combat and endurance, rather than the earlier website’s cap-themed mascot description.

Place in the wider world

The character’s GraffPUNK affiliation does not make him the central ruler of that plural culture. The name carries legend and equipment rumours alongside the practical question of how a fighter survives.

Source traditions

Hash Hammer and Overclock accounts are attributed combat traditions. They do not guarantee an unlimited ability or turn every rumour about his equipment into a universal rule.
