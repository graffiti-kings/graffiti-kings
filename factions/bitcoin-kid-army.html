Bitcoin Kid Army

Army for the crypto market — not a GK street faction

Record type: Army for the crypto market — not a GK street faction.

Read the developed account in Bitcoin Kid Army, or start with the main Crypto Moonboys story.

Identity and role

The Bitcoin Kid Army is the military force for the crypto market. Its undercover guards shadow members of the All City Bulls and Nomad Bears 24/7, staying near both sides of the market even when their protection is invisible.

Alfie “The Bitcoin KiD” Blaze leads the Army and takes orders only from GKniftyHEADS. The Army is not an additional GK street faction and does not take a political seat among the Forty.

Life and practice

The outside Bitcoin Kids community is larger than its enlisted force. Families, repairers, performers and people with no military role belong to that life. Army command does not make every outside child a soldier.

The engineered Bitcoin X Kids are the inside generation in Block Topia. Some escape and join Alfie’s Army. Birth inside the city, belonging to an outside community and choosing military service are distinct relationships.

Command and relationships

GKniftyHEADS stands above the two market factions and the remaining 37 GK factions. When a larger army is required, members of all factions unite as the HODL Warriors; that common force is different from both Alfie’s standing Army and the city’s HODL X Warriors.

The forces face NULL THE PROPHET alongside other threats. Their shared danger does not erase the conflict between the underworld and Queen Sarah P-fly’s controlled city.
