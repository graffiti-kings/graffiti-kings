Identity and role

The NoBallGames Legion is a named culture in the living First Witness register. Its inheritance turns the prohibitive street sign into a question: who gets to play, claim public space and make rules for a shared place?

Older accounts call the Legion the Pitch Lords and connect it to Charlie Buster’s fictional force. That is world-building, distinct from the real artist, NO BALL GAMES brand, $NBG documentation and XRP KIDs project records.

Life and practice

The game can teach fairness, cooperation and skill; it can also let winning become the only measure of a person. The culture’s value lies in keeping play open while taking the effect of its rules seriously.

Its recognised cultural identity does not turn every arena job — scoring, mission design or a timed tag crew — into a separate new political seat.

Command and relationships

Legion members can join the common HODL Warriors army while retaining their faction identity. The Bitcoin Kid Army remains the market’s protection force under Alfie and GKniftyHEADS, not a team to be counted as another street faction.
