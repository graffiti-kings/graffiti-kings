GRAFFITI KINGS / SER: PRESS & WEB NETWORK

A public research trail of 77 curated external source records plus 10 handles declared by the legacy site — independent reporting, broadcast credits, auction records, project channels and social accounts.

How to read this source network

The purpose is discovery, not link-count inflation. Each entry explains why it matters and whether it is journalism, an interview, an auction catalogue, project-authored material or a social handle. A link from this wiki is not proof that the linked site links back here, endorses the wiki, or is still maintained.

This archive is about Darren Cullen, the graffiti artist known as SER, and the Graffiti Kings project. It deliberately excludes unrelated people named Darren Cullen. Dates describe the original publication where known; they do not imply the linked pages were recently updated. If a social service redirects, changes handles or blocks automated access, the legacy site's declared association remains a historical claim rather than proof of current control.

For a researched narrative rather than a directory, read Darren Cullen / SER: documented history, then the wiki biography. The project history and WAX collection guide describe the later movement and its fictional universe.
