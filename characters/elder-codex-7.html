Elder Codex-7

Chain Scribe and Grid Archives keeper

Record type: Fictional character.

In the story

Elder Codex-7 is a Chain Scribe and keeper of the Grid Archives, associated with narration in The Eternal Pulse. His work concerns the preservation, interpretation and transmission of difficult testimony.

Place in the wider world

A keeper’s influence comes from custody and reading, not ownership of the people described in a record. His title does not establish permanent command of all Chain Scribes.

Source traditions

Some narrated dates and completed endings conflict with other witnesses. They remain attributed accounts; the reconciled Year 3008 chronology and unresolved Final Fork govern the public overview.
