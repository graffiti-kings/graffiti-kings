GKniftyHEADS

Leading GK faction and creator identity foundation

Record type: Leading GK faction and creator identity foundation.

Identity and role

GKniftyHEADS has two connected uses. In the real creator ecosystem it carries Graffiti Kings’ identity architecture into characters, digital art and creator worlds. In the Year 3008 fiction, GKniftyHEADS are the underworld good guys who hold authority above the Bulls, Bears and the remaining 37 GK factions.

The political structure is GKniftyHEADS + two main market factions + 37 other GK factions = Forty. This count does not turn every army, royal title, cultural reading or service office into a separate street faction.

Life and practice

The fictional Immortal Architects preserve fragments of civilisation’s memory. Their knowledge gives influence but does not make them rulers of Block Topia. A record can help people survive while also tempting its keeper to control the people recorded.

The real GKniftyHEADS WAX collection is an artefact layer within the wider identity project. A fictional memory ability is not a property promised by an NFT or a substitute for the terms of a qualifying creator licence.

Command and relationships

Alfie “The Bitcoin KiD” Blaze leads the Bitcoin Kid Army and takes orders only from GKniftyHEADS. His army protects members of the All City Bulls and Nomad Bears around the clock.

When a greater force is needed, members of all GK factions unite as the HODL Warriors. They retain their faction identities. GKniftyHEADS’ underworld authority is distinct from Queen Sarah P-fly’s command of Block Topia.

Sources and boundaries

The developed main story and First Witness reading govern current narration. Earlier royal-house and archive-preservation imagery remains a tradition, not an excuse to replace the current command chain or to announce a final victory.
