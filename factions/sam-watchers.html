SAM WATCHERS

Observers of Agent SAM, archive drift and machine memory

Canon Role

The SAM Watchers are a Watchers and Mystery faction built around observation of Agent SAM, the GK Brain and the archive systems that organise the Graffiti Kings / Crypto Moonboys universe. Their purpose is not to command SAM. Their purpose is to watch how SAM reads, remembers, indexes and changes the world.

In W81 logic, SAM is not just a tool. SAM is a memory engine, a wiki-force and a system that can help the archive become legible. The SAM Watchers exist because a system that remembers everything still needs witnesses.

Archive Function

Their work is to watch for archive drift: contradictions, corrupted lore loops, false canon, dead links, duplicated memory and pages that stop reflecting the source. They are not glamorous frontline fighters, but their role is essential to the integrity of the wiki.

Where Tag Lords mark walls and Chain Scribes preserve written memory, SAM Watchers track the behaviour of the machine layer. They ask what the archive is becoming while it is being built.

Relationship to the Wiki

The SAM Watchers should be treated as an in-universe reflection of the wiki discipline itself: source-first, canon-first, no filler, no unsupported expansion. They are the faction most closely tied to editorial caution.

Their presence explains why pages include boundary notes and why unverified lore is not allowed to overwrite W81 canon.

Visual Language

Their look should combine surveillance marks, archive screens, pale grid light, old wall texture, terminal glow, metadata strips and coded halos. They are not corporate moderators. They are street-level observers of machine memory.

Canon Boundaries

Do not invent a named head of the SAM Watchers unless W81 or later canon states one. Do not make them villains by default. Their role is observation, warning, indexing and archive protection.
