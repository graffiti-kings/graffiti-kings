CRYPTO MOONBOYS / GKNIFTYHEADS / GRAFFPUNKS

A source-linked map of the contemporary creator umbrella, street-culture roots, character rights, WAX art, radio, games and the fictional 3008 universe.

Four names, four different functions

The latest public Crypto Moonboys home and About page describe the wider project as a creator-identity umbrella rather than a single token or NFT drop. Their declared structure can be read as four overlapping, but distinct, functions:

  • Graffiti Kings: the London street-art history and cultural foundation. The old commercial website points to the contemporary digital successor; see the documented SER history.
  • GKniftyHEADS: the primary Moonboy character and identity foundation, with related WAX NFT assets and its fictional Immortal Architects storyline.
  • GraffPUNKS: the cultural broadcast network — radio, writing, art and publishing — plus a separate rebellion role in the Year 3008 fiction.
  • Crypto Moonboys: the public umbrella for identity worlds, approved creator work, wiki knowledge, factions, optional games, and the associated tools.

For the project’s own fuller explanations, compare Graffiti Kings wiki, GKniftyHEADS wiki, GraffPUNKS wiki and Crypto Moonboys wiki. These are official first-party descriptions, not independent validation of every historical or commercial claim.

Two public creator paths, not a mandatory NFT purchase

The current official model gives a creator two routes: develop a new Moonboy/Flygirl character world, or retain an existing IP, artist name or business identity and connect its own lore and creative output to the wider universe. The second route does not require the user to first mint an NFT, choose a faction or earn Arcade XP. The first can make use of Telegram and game progression where a supported route is published.

Current About page and Graffiti Kings Creative Guide emphasise recognisable work and documented proof rather than a universal membership token. This is a description of the project's published offer, not a guarantee of an audience, revenue or public promotion.

What a qualifying 1/1 character grants — and what it does not

The latest GKniftyHEADS source draws a firm boundary: qualifying unique 1/1 Moonboy identities may have specific creator licences and related terms, but ordinary NFT editions, earlier rewards, templates, game items and generic digital art do not automatically inherit those rights. Creator rights and official story canon are separate processes. Consult the relevant published creator terms for the actual scope, permissions and transfer conditions.

The current source also treats HODL Warriors as a formal creator-builder role associated with qualifying 1/1 holders; it is not an ordinary selectable faction. Older Substack promotional pages and fictional faction histories use “HODL Warriors” much more broadly. Those older statements are evidence of how the project was promoted at the time, not a replacement for the current terms. Current GKniftyHEADS record; September 2025 faction article.

Creator Studio, SWARMSY and SPARKY — public versus private

The official creative brief explains how a distinctive mark can grow through an approved story, legal physical artwork, digital proof and an owned archive. The public SPARKY/ChatGPT guide provides copy-ready prompts for that method. These public pages do not imply that an external visitor can open private project records or make an autonomous bot publish content.

The current project About page describes SWARMSY as a separate optional PC-based creator environment. Some older pages, including SPARKY AI Chat, describe a Telegram-gated public bridge; its current delivery and availability should be tested rather than assumed from its text. the May 2026 SWARMSY manifesto is a vision/development article, not evidence that every proposed offline mesh or humanoid feature exists.

Wiki, Arcade, Telegram and Block Topia

The Moonboys Arcade hub lists games and shared score/XP features, with Telegram identity used for competitive progression and server-accepted XP. Its public description distinguishes score-based ranks from accepted XP and advertises a Block Topia gate. As gameplay, beta names and earning conditions can change, the site itself remains the live reference for rules and supported games.

The wiki categories and Crypto Moonboys umbrella record help readers move from game pages to character lore, WAX collection history and identity tools. The Graffiti Kings archive records those connections rather than presenting a game's advertised rewards as an independently guaranteed payout.

GraffPUNKS: the live broadcast and the written archive

GraffPUNKS.live presents itself as an online music station with DJs, house, techno, hip hop, grime and related genres. The Crypto Moonboys radio wiki supplies background and historical DJ names; the live station site should be consulted for current broadcasting and schedules. Read the local music and radio chapter for the earlier Graffiti Kings Radio directory records.

The written network spans GraffPUNKS Substack, GKniftyHEADS Medium, and GKniftyHEADS YouTube. A dated May 2026 subject register labels the movement's different fictional characters, project entities and publication arms. The Immortal Architects article is creative fiction, not a verified account of real future events.

WAX artefacts, catalogue identities and historical commercial plans

WAX provides the published blockchain provenance and marketplace layer for many of the project’s NFTs. NFT Hive and other marketplaces can provide current collection listings, but captions, prices, trading volume and active offers are time-dependent.

A separately published Invasion Toys designer-toy/NFT announcement is a historic commercial collaboration around Bitcoin Kid, Crypto Moonboys and physical-redemption ideas. The separate NO BALL GAMES / $NBG branch

Charlie Buster's NO BALL GAMES is a connected but independent sign-flip art universe. Its WAX $NBG fan-token proposals, WAX noballgamess NFT collection, XRPL XRP KIDs and 2035 fiction must be kept distinct from GKniftyHEADS' qualifying 1/1 licences and the wider Crypto Moonboys Arcade. The real art record and public-source index provide external context. TREEF's published regeneration ideas are addressed in a cautious separate research entry.

The Year 3008 mythos is intentionally different from real history

The Graffiti Kings wiki’s faction pages, GraffPUNKS Paint Path, Immortal Architects and Block Topia describe a shared speculative future. Jodie Zoom's GraffPUNKS narrative and the Intergalactic Federation transmission are useful creative-source trails. Their fictional characters, timelines and invented chain events are not real history or on-chain facts.

A separate community-edited GKniftyHEADS Fandom page republishes an interpretation of this universe. It is a discovery lead, not an authoritative statement of current licence rights, released products or verified third-party endorsements. Compare anything consequential against the maintained official site, and clearly label any imported fan interpretation.
