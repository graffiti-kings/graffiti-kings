Crypto Moongirls

Queen Sarah P-fly’s Block Topia army

Record type: Queen Sarah P-fly’s Block Topia army.

Read the developed account in Crypto Moongirls, or start with the main Crypto Moonboys story.

Identity and role

The Crypto Moongirls are Queen Sarah P-fly’s ruling military and administrative force in Block Topia. “Eternal” survives in this site’s inherited title and URL; the current role is the city’s army and order, not an interchangeable underworld street faction.

Their discipline helps keep a surviving city supplied and governed. It also supports the systems that monitor, classify and recruit its residents and visitors. Protection and control coexist in their institutions.

Life and practice

The Hard Fork Games bring candidates toward the city’s promises. Clone Harvest and neural-copy accounts ask what those promises conceal, who can refuse and whose memory an institution may use.

Moongirls are not one undifferentiated will. Thera-9 defects and helps the Punk Net; other members remain within the order. Jodie’s “Echo Mother” title belongs to a specific synthetic splinter, not biological motherhood or ownership of the whole army.

Command and relationships

Queen Sarah P-fly rules the city with this army and its HODL X Warriors. The engineered Bitcoin X Kids are the inside generation; some escape to join Alfie’s outside Bitcoin Kid Army.

The GKniftyHEADS-led underworld has its own command structure and united HODL Warriors. Facing NULL together does not make those forces part of a single settled government.

Sources and boundaries

The developed Moongirls account and controlled reading of the First Witness explain their political and cultural pressures. The real Graffiti Queens art exhibitions remain distinct historical events, not military episodes in this fiction.
