Story Mechanics and Participation

Faction identity, military service, the Games and the limits of a record.

Story institutions and participation

This page explains how the updated world is organised and how its story concepts differ from live project features. A ritual in fiction, a proposed game design and a deployed service are different things. The current main lore supplies the story spine.

Walls, identity and witness

The street gives the world its first record: a mark that says somebody was here. Reputation can protect a maker, while a wall can also be claimed in ways that exclude others. A surviving mark must still be read beside its living subject.

The First Witness’s Wall, Hold, Fork and Keeper are interpreted across different cultures. No single faction’s favourite principle replaces the nine Books or excuses all harm done in its name.

Different chains, different responsibilities

The Sacred / Aether-Chain concerns Covenant history, custody and contested memory. The World Chain suffers the Triple Fork in 2880. Block Topia’s True Bitcoin Fork supports its controlled order. These are distinct histories and functions.

SoulTags and memory repair are fictional institutions with limits. A record is not automatically a whole person; loss does not imply a universal restore function; every human is not established as uploaded software.

Faction identity and Army service

In Year 3008, crypto rules everyday life. The All City Bulls and Nomad Bears embody the market’s two sides. Both are shadowed 24/7 by undercover Bitcoin Kid Army guards. Alfie “The Bitcoin KiD” Blaze leads that Army and takes orders only from GKniftyHEADS, the underworld good guys above the two market factions and the remaining 37 GK factions.

When the underworld needs a larger army, members of all GK factions unite as the HODL Warriors while keeping their faction identities. The Bitcoin Kid Army and HODL Warriors are armies, not street factions. Every HODL Warrior aspires to become a HODL X Warrior of Block Topia, but that distinct status must be earned.

Seeding, selection and earned status

Seeding accounts belong to local Hard Fork and champion traditions. They should be read with the source’s rules and costs, not as a blanket real-world grant of territory, reproductive rights, ownership or rewards.

Every HODL Warrior aspires to become a HODL X Warrior. Wanting that status does not prove selection, equipment, privileges or completed trials.

Hard Fork Games

Block Topia summons candidates from the Forty. Competition offers entry, recognition and security while making outsiders legible to the Authority. Feast Week, trials, Clone Harvest, escape and later service each have their own accounts.

Read the Games record for the promises, obligations and lives beyond the rankings. The recurring Games are not the same event as the World Chain Triple Fork.

Common defence without a settled ending

All face NULL THE PROPHET, the Antichain threat that can sever a record from the life it should preserve. Other wars, rivalries and obligations continue. A common enemy does not settle the struggle between the street and the Queen, and the public story leaves the Final Fork unresolved.

HODL WARS follows the cost of coalition, contested bargains and losses that cannot simply be repaired by a new upload. A proposed boss encounter or later serial victory does not settle the public Final Fork.

Burn, earn and own: source-specific terms

Burn paths, upgrades and earning loops appear in older project and game concepts. Their appearance in lore does not show that a contract, item conversion or reward is available now. A live feature needs its own current documentation.

Creator use also needs the applicable licence. A qualifying 1/1 character’s permissions, a purchased NFT, faction membership and the right to rewrite central canon are different matters. See the connected creator guide.

Tokens and the real project record

Older archive concepts attach symbolic action and commitment roles to $PUNK and $STAKE. This mythology does not set live supply, APR, redemption, price or payout terms.

WAXP is the native WAX asset, separate from GK-issued or connected project tokens. $NBG and XRP KIDs belong to their own documented branches. Real financial or service terms must come from the relevant current source.

Relics, scrolls and custody

A Codex relic or scroll can carry testimony, an identity or a local story key. Its presence in fiction is not proof of a minted asset or a live unlock. Name the specific object, witness and limits before treating it as a rule.

The Sacred Chain and Justice, Mercy and Restitution ask how evidence can remain useful without making people captives of a record.

Proposed games and live participation

Blockfront, DreamWars and Forklords contain proposed encounters and progression concepts. Story accounts about Forklord You or Samael.exe do not demonstrate released cross-game tracking or rewards.

The real Arcade, Telegram and creator tools have their own interfaces and rules. Follow the project site and the source-linked network guide for those routes rather than inferring availability from lore.

SAM, the GK Grid and archive intelligence

The GK Grid includes tactical and cultural coordination; it should not be reduced to a single surveillance villain. SAM’s fictional memory roles and modern assistant tools remain separately described.

An assistant can retrieve and draft material without gaining power to approve it as canon. A fictional upload, a rebuilt memory and a current software deployment need different evidence.

How the records fit together

GK’s explicit current canon and the reconciled First Witness framework govern current narration. The source register, master chronology and Concordance record how earlier material is retained without making every contradiction true at once.

W81, serials, codices and older website interpretations remain witnesses. An older ending, date, title or origin stays attached to its source unless reconciled. The nine Books of the First Witness are not rewritten by a modern page summary. Real history, a creator licence, proposed gameplay and fictional events each need their own evidence.
