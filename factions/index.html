Factions, Armies and Traditions

The market factions, the underworld’s common service and Block Topia’s distinct order.

The current structure

The story centres on the All City Bulls and Nomad Bears, the market’s two main factions in a world ruled by crypto. GKniftyHEADS are the underworld good guys above those two and the remaining 37 GK factions: one leading faction + two market factions + 37 others = Forty.

The Bitcoin Kid Army is the army for the crypto market. Its undercover guards shadow Bulls and Bears 24/7. Alfie “The Bitcoin KiD” Blaze commands it and takes orders only from GKniftyHEADS.

HODL Warriors are the underworld army formed from all factions’ members, who retain their identities. They are not a faction. Every HODL Warrior aspires to become a distinct HODL X Warrior in Block Topia, ruled by Queen Sarah P-fly with her Crypto Moongirls army.

The city’s engineered Bitcoin X Kids are the inside generation. Some escape and join Alfie’s Army outside. Birth, community membership and enlistment remain separate. A common struggle against NULL does not remove every conflict between the city and the underworld.

Main factions and military forces

  • GKniftyHEADS — Leading GK faction and creator identity foundation.
  • All City Bulls — One of the two main market factions.
  • Nomad Bears — One of the two main market factions.
  • Bitcoin Kid Army — Army for the crypto market — not a GK street faction.
  • HODL Warriors — United underworld army — not a faction.
  • HODL X Warriors — Block Topia champion and specialist-service order.
  • Crypto Moongirls — Queen Sarah P-fly’s Block Topia army.

Other named cultures and households

These names have their own cultural or household accounts. A culture, a family, an office, a military order and a political seat are not the same kind of membership. The First Witness commentary register preserves overlapping traditions; it is not a one-to-one list of the Forty’s governmental seats.

  • GraffPUNKS — GK street faction and plural Paint Path culture.
  • NoBallGames Legion — GK faction culture with a separate real artist-brand connection.
  • House of Rackinsats — Fictional household and mutual-aid tradition.
  • Whale Lords — Named culture of long holding and economic memory.
  • Wildstyle Collective — Named culture in the living First Witness register.
  • Fractal Taggers — Named culture in the living First Witness register.
