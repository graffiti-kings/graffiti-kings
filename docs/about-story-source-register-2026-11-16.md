## Editorial approach

The historical argument, discussion of craft, interpretation of objects and account of how an archive can serve creators are original narrative commentary. They should not be mistaken for reconstructed scenes or quotations from named people. No invented station chronology, exact relocation year, private family biography, legal result, financial return or guaranteed technical capability is added.

The four-decade span refers to the graffiti roots from 1983, not a claim that one agency or legal entity traded continuously throughout that period. The supplied text’s historical financial metrics, absolute blockchain claims, superseded faction labels, company-dissolution assertion and future-feature promises are not repeated as verified current facts.

Real people and their fictional roles remain distinct. GKniftyHEADS commands the underworld structure; Alfie’s Bitcoin Kid Army protects the market and is not a street faction. HODL Warriors combine all factions’ members, retain those identities and aspire to the separate HODL X order under Queen Sarah P-fly and the Crypto Moongirls. Inside Bitcoin X Kids, outside community and Army enlistment remain different categories.
