Loopfiend

Repeater of Regret

Record type: Fictional character.

Read the developed account in Loopfiend, or start with the main Crypto Moonboys story.

In the story

Loopfiend is the Repeater of Regret in Infinite Alley 9x, an encounter built around failed correction and repetition. Its face can resemble the traveller who keeps trying to repair the same error.

Place in the wider world

The figure makes a local trap out of remorse: another attempt can preserve the problem as easily as solve it. That is a story condition, not proof that the entire multiverse is one repeating simulation.

Source traditions

The Infinite Alley account does not give Loopfiend rule over all chronology, every timeline door or the outcomes of the Year 3008 war.
