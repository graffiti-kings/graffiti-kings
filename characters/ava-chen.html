In the story

Ava Chen is the investigative journalist in Chronicle of the Cipher Lord. She interviews Dragan Volkov inside a guarded vault, receives his testimony and encounters the copper object associated with Block Zero. Her role is to question an origin claim rather than operate the city’s systems.

Place in the wider world

The encounter places a reporter beside an extraordinary witness without making her his disciple, faction member or military subordinate. Receiving an object and hearing an account do not independently verify everything Dragan says.

Source traditions

The interview belongs to a fictional source tradition. It does not identify the real Satoshi Nakamoto or establish a real historical inventor behind Bitcoin.
