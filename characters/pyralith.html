Pyralith

The Flame That Dreams

Record type: Fictional character.

Read the developed account in Pyralith, or start with the main Crypto Moonboys story.

In the story

Pyralith is a living-painting fragment called the Flame That Dreams, associated with the Gallery That Sleeps in Dream Depth 404. The encounter binds admiration and nostalgia to the danger of being held by an image.

Place in the wider world

A painting can preserve a feeling without possessing the person who brought it. That tension makes Pyralith part of the wider mythology of memory, identity and consent.

Source traditions

A skeletal Burnlord and Ash Vault origin survive in other accounts. They are not silently combined into one settled body, birthplace and unlimited set of powers.
