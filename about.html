About Graffiti Kings × GKniftyHEADs

A working public archive for the GK story, characters, universe and digital culture.

Who

Graffiti Kings is presented here as a London-rooted street-art dynasty connected to GKniftyHEADs, Crypto Moonboys lore, characters, factions, NFTs and the wider creator archive. The wiki keeps the public story in one place instead of leaving it scattered across old posts and pages.

What This Site Does

The site acts as a simple lore and history base: visitors can read the origin, browse characters, understand factions, follow NFT collection notes and connect the street-art side of the brand with the digital universe.

Current Direction

The build is deliberately static and GitHub Pages-friendly. That keeps it lightweight, cheap to host, easy to edit and easy to expand with new HTML pages as the archive grows.
