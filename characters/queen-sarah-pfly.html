Queen Sarah P-fly

Ruler of Block Topia · Crypto Moongirls command

Record type: Fictional character.

Read the developed account in Queen Sarah P-fly, or start with the main Crypto Moonboys story.

In the story

Queen Sarah P-fly rules Block Topia with her Crypto Moongirls army. Her protected city offers working food, water, power and identity systems in a fractured world. That achievement makes her authority persuasive: residents have practical reasons to value a city that can keep them alive.

The same order classifies, recruits and controls people. The Hard Fork Games bring candidates from the Forty into the city seeking security and status, while giving the Authority access to lives beyond its walls. Stories of neural copying and the Clone Harvest turn the promise of belonging into a struggle over consent and memory.

Place in the wider world

The HODL X Warriors belong to the city’s champion and specialist-service order. The underworld’s united HODL Warriors aspire to their status, but the two forces have different command, selection and obligations.

The city’s Bitcoin X Kids are the engineered inside generation. Some escape to join Alfie’s outside Bitcoin Kid Army. Jodie, Aleema and Thera-9 reveal different kinds of refusal; none of their testimonies makes every resident or Moongirl identical.

NULL THE PROPHET threatens the city and the outside world. Shared resistance to NULL leaves unresolved the Queen’s conflict with the streets. No public account supplies a final, universal victory.

Source traditions

Queen Sarah is a fictional role. Sarah PU51FLY’s real-root identity and the documented 2021 Graffiti Queens exhibition have separate records. Participating artists in that exhibition are not automatically characters, commanders or creators of the Queen.
