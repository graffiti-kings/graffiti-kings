LOST MOONBOYS

The displaced echoes, missing holders and forgotten signal of the Moonboy line

Canon Role

The Lost Moonboys are a mystery faction inside the W81 structure. They represent those who fell out of the main signal: forgotten holders, dead wallets, abandoned avatars, missing lore fragments, displaced members and Moonboy traces left behind between versions of the universe.

They are not outside the canon. They are proof that the canon has shadows. Every large archive produces gaps. Every chain has dormant wallets. Every street movement has people who were there before the page was written. The Lost Moonboys carry that absence.

Archive Function

Their role is to keep the wiki honest about forgotten material. Not every figure has a full record. Not every holder remained visible. Not every story reached the main faction tables. The Lost Moonboys give the universe a place for unresolved memory without forcing it into false certainty.

They can be used as a future expansion space for recovered lore, old wallets, missing characters, abandoned drops or resurrected archive fragments, but only when source support exists.

Relationship to the Main Moonboy Line

The Lost Moonboys mirror the main Crypto Moonboys identity from the edge. Where the main Moonboys are visible in the brand spine, the Lost Moonboys are the hidden underside: half-remembered, glitched, incomplete and waiting for confirmation.

Visual Language

Use faded helmets, broken moon icons, half-rendered avatars, archive dust, dead-wallet glow, old metadata, ghost PFP outlines and street tags that look found rather than freshly painted.

Canon Boundaries

Do not use this page to invent random lost heroes. The Lost Moonboys are a controlled archive category for missing, dormant or unresolved Moonboy memory.
