Billy the Goat Kid

Outsider chronicler and lore herald

Record type: Fictional character.

Read the developed account in Billy the Goat Kid, or start with the main Crypto Moonboys story.

In the story

Billy is an outsider chronicler and lore herald, carrying rhyme and code through patched street armour and a sonic-scroll tradition. He brings a voice to the story without holding an established place in its military command.

Place in the wider world

His outsider position matters: a narrator can salute a culture, carry a warning or retell a battle without governing the people involved. He is not simply a rooftop mascot or an Army recruit by default.

Source traditions

No confirmed alliance follows from his salute to Team Banksy. That fictional reference is not evidence of a real-world association or a commission from Banksy.
