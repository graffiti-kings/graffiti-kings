ZIP Truth

The W81 archive names House of Rackinsats Eternal as one of the original eight Core London Bloodline factions.

It is described as the living echo of Denise Aethelgard, Tracey Vara, and Papa Des.

The archive calls it the spiritual safe zone and the Eternal Porch faction.

It says this faction does not fight; it holds the family together.

Every faction sends representatives here on every major cycle to remember who they really are.
