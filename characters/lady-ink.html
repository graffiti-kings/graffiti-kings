Lady-INK

Permanent-record artist

Record type: Fictional character.

Read the developed account in Lady-INK, or start with the main Crypto Moonboys story.

In the story

Lady-INK is a fictional artist concerned with the permanent record, remembered in black tracksuit, trainers and paint rucksack. Echo Ink and dream traditions connect her marks to the struggle over what survives erasure.

Place in the wider world

Her art belongs to the same wider question as the Chain Scribes’ custody: who is preserved by a record, who is left out, and whether a lasting image can respect its living subject.

Source traditions

Dream accounts and permanent-mark language do not establish a guaranteed power to save every person, undo every loss or deliver a universal final victory.
