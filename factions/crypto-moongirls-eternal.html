ZIP Truth

The W81 archive names Crypto Moongirls Eternal as one of the original eight Core London Bloodline factions.

They are led by Queen Sarah P-fly.

The archive describes them as elegant, fractal, and terrifyingly efficient.

They maintain the Optimisation Layer and run the HARD FORK Program.

They are described as the necessary sky to the streets’ chaos.
