## Scope and method

This research concerns the older `graffitikings.co.uk` commercial site, **not** the modern fictional W81 / Year 3008 lore or current commercial services. Record the publisher, date and evidence level separately.

**Access caveat:** Wayback URLs below were surfaced in earlier archive inspection or third-party published references. During this update the Wayback host did not allow direct replay through the available research interface, so **individual page rendering, images and HTTP response status were not freshly verified**. Do not represent this as an exhaustive CDX enumeration or a complete downloadable backup. Historic campaign descriptions trace to the earlier archive review, not a new independent confirmation. Readers can use the archive's snapshot selector to try adjacent dates.

Do not reproduce copyrighted mural photos or original site paragraphs. The site presents editorial summaries with links and exact provenance, not a mirrored copy.
