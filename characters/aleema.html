Aleema

Hard Fork Games survivor · Forkborn

Record type: Fictional character.

Read the developed account in Aleema, or start with the main Crypto Moonboys story.

In the story

Aleema escapes Block Topia with Jodie ZOOM 2000 and an unnamed third woman after rejecting the terms offered to female Games recruits. She belongs to the Forkborn founding refusal and the Shard Mothers of Manhattan tradition.

Her distinctive work is crossing languages as well as boundaries. Accounts place her between GraffPUNK cultural speech and chainlight command, able to hear when the same promise commits its listeners to different things. She accompanies, questions and helps others make a passage rather than simply repeating Jodie’s visions.

Place in the wider world

The Games arrival scene places Aleema alongside Jodie, apart from the celebrating male champions. Hope of becoming a Moongirl has real force before they understand the price of admission. Her escape challenges the city’s control over the meaning of belonging.

The Forkborn’s connection to the Shard Mothers is not a newly invented extra political seat. A cultural affiliation, an earned title and membership in a wartime army describe different relationships.

Source traditions

“Child of the Shard” is an inherited epithet. It does not establish Aleema’s age, birth mechanism or identity as a separate prophetic child in Eternal Pulse visions. The archive has no reconciled family tree for her.
