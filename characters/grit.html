GRIT

Flame of Forgotten Graffiti · Dreamcore Burnlord

Record type: Fictional character.

Read the developed account in GRIT, or start with the main Crypto Moonboys story.

In the story

GRIT is the female Flame of Forgotten Graffiti, a Dreamcore Burnlord associated with the Firetrain in Loop Layer 33. Her story treats an erased or burned mural as a source of dangerous remembered fire.

Place in the wider world

She belongs to a particular Dreamcore tradition, where place, memory and an encounter’s conditions matter. This is not a generic biography of every hard survivor called Grit.

Source traditions

A burned-mural account names 2055. Other portrayals describe a male survivor; that version remains separate. GRIT and the EVM Punks warrior Grit42 are distinct characters.
