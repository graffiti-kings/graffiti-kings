Mutant Pasteups

W81 Faction 35 — Hybrid street fragments that refuse clean form

Faction Overview

Mutant Pasteups are a hybrid tribe formed from layered posters, damaged street graphics, torn lore fragments and recycled visual memory. They are not clean avatars. They are collage-beings: half message, half wall damage, half character, half warning. Their existence keeps the universe close to real street surfaces, where paper, wheatpaste, weather and time change the art after the artist leaves.

Inside the W81 faction system, Mutant Pasteups represent mutation through repetition. A paste-up goes up, gets torn, gets covered, gets photographed, gets copied, gets printed again and returns changed. That cycle is their spiritual engine.

Role in the Grid

Mutant Pasteups spread culture through reproduction rather than command. They appear in alleys, on message boards, under bridge nodes, across arcade walls and inside abandoned data kiosks. They are one of the ways the Grid talks to itself without an official broadcast.

They often carry fragments from other factions: a GraffPUNK slogan, a Moongirl pattern, a HODL warning, a NoBallGames match symbol, a Porch memory. The mutation happens when those fragments overlap until they become a new signal.

Street Function

Their power is cultural persistence. A mural can be buffed. A message can be censored. But pasteups multiply fast, appear in clusters and survive through copies. The faction turns cheap material into mass mythology.

Their visual language is torn paper, crooked edges, repeated icons, photocopy grain, spray overs, wheatpaste drips, missing faces and layered typography. They keep the W81 world rough.

Canon Boundaries

This page treats Mutant Pasteups as W81 canon: a hybrid tribe of layered, mutable street fragments. Extra leaders, wars, supply claims or mechanics should not be added unless they come from the archive or later approved canon.
