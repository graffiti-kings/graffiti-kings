BITCOIN KID, INVASION TOYS & CRYPTO MOONBOYS

The externally published Graffiti Kings physical-to-digital collectible plans, separated from verified minting, actual sales and shipping history.

A named external toy-company collaboration

Invasion Toys published two articles about a Graffiti Kings collaboration involving Bitcoin Kid, a physical vinyl character and Crypto Moonboys NFT artwork. The publisher's own website is evidence that a real external toy company publicly promoted the partnership. It is more specific than a generic list of proposed “phygital” products. The relevant pages are the OG Bitcoin Kid announcement and the updated NFT/physical-token plan.

This archive distinguishes an announced physical figure, an intended NFT redemption and delivered customer orders. Neither article is a contemporary production audit or independently verified shipment report. Future availability, refunds, licence terms and quantities must be checked using primary transaction or release records before being stated as current facts.

The published dates and changing proposals

Invasion Toys currently labels its OG Bitcoin Kid article 4 August 2024, while the page titled Bitcoin Kid NFT + Physical Token Project — Revamped and Rescheduled is labelled 26 August 2023. The older-dated article describes a revised project relative to earlier plans, so the displayed dates do not form a clean sequential revision history. A visitor should read the original pages rather than assume the current archive listing dates identify the order of product decisions.

The OG Bitcoin Kid article describes a planned vinyl figure partnership, a digital-art add-on option and an unreleased turn-based Crypto Moonboys game. The update changes the emphasis toward buying WAX NFT characters and, at higher rarity tiers, exchanging or burning some for physical objects. This is a history of advertised concepts and revisions. 10,000 proposed Bitcoin Kid NFTs and character layers

The update proposed 10,000 distinct Bitcoin Kid NFTs on WAX for a Crypto Moonboys game and mentioned further future plans for Ethereum. It also listed seven possible visual layers — clothing, chain, face, hat, weapon, pet and background — with changing asset counts as development continued. Those numbers represent a design goal and generator inventory as described in the announcement, not a verified number of blockchain assets minted or in circulation today.

The pages described a proposed turn-based character game with equipment and pets. That advertised concept is historically relevant to the current Crypto Moonboys Arcade, but the modern Arcade must not be represented as the same game unless software evidence explicitly establishes continuity. See the WAX collection-account directory for public identifiers and independent marketplace lookup links.

Proposed vinyl and resin redemption tiers

Invasion Toys described standard Bitcoin Kid vinyl variants and special hand-painted vinyl or resin Bitcoin Pepe figures as intended physical rewards for certain NFT rarities. The update advertised 1,000 Legendary units, proposed ten vinyl variants of 100 each, plus five hand-painted vinyl and five resin pieces for a higher tier. This is a source-reported proposed design, not a confirmed manufacturing or redemption count.

The 2023 article is internally inconsistent about the top rarity: it describes 10 hyper-legendary NFTs out of 10,000 (which would be 0.1%) but elsewhere labels that figure 0.01%. The correct arithmetic does not validate that the 10 assets were ever minted. We preserve the discrepancy as a reason to check the underlying collection and any later contract rules. Release dates, sales and delivery must be independently checked

The revised article announced a move to a 3 September drop at 9 a.m. Pacific and quoted a proposed $50-equivalent purchase in WAX. Neither the named date nor the advertised price is an offer live today. Some earlier combinations were discussed as free accompanying NFTs or priced add-ons, showing why a one-line “Bitcoin Kid launch price” is misleading.

Before publishing realised revenue, production quantities, shipping completion, sold-out claims or collector redemption status, find a later publisher statement, order fulfilment archive or specific on-chain issuance/burn records. No such outcome is asserted on this page based solely on the two Invasion Toys announcements. Original announcement · Revised announcement.

Links into the wider Graffiti Kings record

Bitcoin Kid is part of the real-world Graffiti Kings digital-art and designer-toy story, while the character's Alfie Bitcoin Kid and the Bitcoin Kid Army are developed inside the fictional Year 3008 wiki. These should not be merged into one biographical character. The same studio also appears in a 2021 Decentraland exhibition record and SYNC and Alien Worlds collaboration records.
