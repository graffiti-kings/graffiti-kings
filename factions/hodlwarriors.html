HODL Warriors

United underworld army — not a faction

Record type: United underworld army — not a faction.

Read the developed account in HODL Warriors, or start with the main Crypto Moonboys story.

Identity and role

The HODL Warriors are the underworld army formed from members of all GK factions. When a greater force is needed, the factions unite for common service. Members keep their original faction identities; the muster does not create a forty-first faction.

This is the collective force of the underworld, rather than a separate diamond-hands tribe or a generic term for anybody who owns tokens. It can include people whose cultures, loyalties and views of the city remain different.

Life and practice

Common service requires coordination without pretending that every disagreement has ended. Food, routes, equipment, testimony and care matter alongside combat. Answering the call does not erase a household’s needs or give an army ownership of its members.

Every HODL Warrior aspires to become a HODL X Warrior. Aspiration makes the city’s promise powerful even for people who resist its authority. It does not mean that admission, training or the Hard Fork trials have already been completed.

Command and relationships

The HODL X Warriors belong to Block Topia under Queen Sarah P-fly and the Crypto Moongirls army. The underworld muster and the city’s elite order have different service obligations and must not be used as interchangeable names.

The Bitcoin Kid Army remains Alfie’s market-protection force, taking orders only from GKniftyHEADS. The united HODL army and Block Topia’s forces confront NULL THE PROPHET while carrying conflicts of their own.

Sources and boundaries

The Hard Fork Games and HODL WARS give the broader setting. Earlier “faction” labels on this site are superseded. The older Whale Lords War Arc remains a source tradition, not proof that the common army is a separate political faction or that the Final Fork is complete.
