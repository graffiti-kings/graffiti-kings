The Princess

Rebuilding leader in Frequency of the Fallen

Record type: Fictional character.

Read the developed account in The Princess, or start with the main Crypto Moonboys story.

In the story

The Princess appears most concretely as a leader helping rebuild Block City in Frequency of the Fallen. Once a figurehead of rebellion, she participates in the common work after the struggle against the Controllers.

Place in the wider world

That scene gives a title practical weight without establishing a hereditary claim, complete royal court or permanent command of everyone who fought beside her.

Source traditions

The title also occurs in dream catalogues, royal legends and the devotional tradition of Princess Tracey Vara. The sources do not establish that the unnamed leader, Tracey, Rya-B and Queen Sarah P-fly are one person. The rebuilding account does not settle the Final Fork.
