NFT Collections and Character Artefacts

The real collection record beside its fictional meanings.

Art, identity and source records

NFTs can carry art, provenance and a creator identity within the real project ecosystem. Fiction gives such objects further meanings as records, relics or character artefacts. The two layers connect without every fictional ability becoming a feature of a real token.

The WAX collection record

The real Graffiti Kings and GKniftyHEADS art history includes named WAX collection accounts. Use the seven-collection source register for identifiers and the WAXP guide for native network infrastructure. A collection account is not a military unit or political seat.

Earlier collection lists

The older site grouped five archive-referenced collection names. That was a partial historical list, not a current limit on the ecosystem. The source-attributed register below records seven identifiers and preserves the distinction between project labels, marketplace accounts and announcements.

Claims and participation

Free claims, grinding and reward loops appear in historical programme descriptions and game concepts. A lore page does not guarantee that a claim is currently available, that participation will earn a token or that every reader has the same eligibility.

Burn paths and proposed upgrades

An older burn-to-upgrade concept must remain labelled as its own programme or proposal. The fiction’s sacrifice and transformation imagery does not demonstrate an active contract or a guaranteed conversion.

Physical art and collectible announcements

Physical objects and NFT-related plans need dated product evidence. The Bitcoin Kid × Invasion Toys archive distinguishes published plans from proof of delivery. A fictional army’s name does not establish a delivered toy line.

Separate assets and projects

WAXP is the native WAX asset. Other project names and tokens have separate histories, documentation and terms. The NO BALL GAMES record keeps WAX $NBG distinct from the XRPL XRP KIDs branch.

A brand, a cast and a wider world

Crypto Moonboys is a creator brand and a fantasy-world cast. Real people can make, inhabit and perform characters, as performers bring a film’s cast to life. The story grows from Graffiti Kings’ street-art roots into many realms, divided records and a struggle over who gets to own a name, a memory or a future.

The creator umbrella extends beyond any one collection. A real person can bring a character to life without every act becoming central canon; a qualifying licence has its own scope. Read the connected creator guide for those routes.

Holder, performer, faction member and soldier

These are different relationships. Holding an artefact does not automatically enlist someone in Alfie’s Army, grant HODL X status or make every piece of lore they publish approved. Members of the GK factions can join the united HODL Warriors while keeping their political identity.

The engineered Bitcoin X Kids in Block Topia are fictional people, not a blanket description of NFT holders or all player accounts. A stored record does not guarantee recovery of a lost person.

WAXP: the native network token, not a GK-issued asset

WAXP powers the WAX blockchain used for Graffiti Kings, GKniftyHEADS and other WAX-native NFT accounts. It is distinct from project-issued assets such as LFGK or NBG and distinct from the NFTs themselves. The new guide explains WAX's CPU, NET, RAM and PowerUp resources, AtomicAssets collections/schemas/templates, staking, governance and the permissions needed for marketplace actions. A published NFT promise is not proof of present utility or a transferable reward.

How the records fit together

GK’s explicit current canon and the reconciled First Witness framework govern current narration. The source register, master chronology and Concordance record how earlier material is retained without making every contradiction true at once.

W81, serials, codices and older website interpretations remain witnesses. An older ending, date, title or origin stays attached to its source unless reconciled. The nine Books of the First Witness are not rewritten by a modern page summary. Real history, a creator licence, proposed gameplay and fictional events each need their own evidence.
