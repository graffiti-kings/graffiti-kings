Alfie “The Bitcoin KiD” Blaze

Leader of the Bitcoin Kid Army · Year 3008

Record type: Fictional character.

Read the developed account in Alfie “The Bitcoin KiD” Blaze, or start with the main Crypto Moonboys story.

In the story

Alfie leads the Bitcoin Kid Army, the military force protecting the crypto market. He is a commander, fighter, teacher and rebel signal. He takes orders only from GKniftyHEADS, the underworld good guys above the two main market factions and the remaining 37 GK factions.

The All City Bulls and Nomad Bears embody the bulls and bears in a world where crypto rules everyday life. Alfie’s undercover guards shadow their members 24/7. Protection stays close even when it cannot be seen. The Bitcoin Kid Army is an army, not a GK street faction or an extra seat in the Forty.

Alfie leads escaped and outside Bitcoin Kids, but the outside community is wider than its enlisted force. Carers, makers, couriers and people who refuse military service also belong to that life. His authority as commander does not make him owner of their households or spokesman for every Bitcoin Bloodline story.

Place in the wider world

When the underworld needs a larger force, members of all GK factions unite as the HODL Warriors. This combined army retains its members’ faction identities. It is distinct from Block Topia’s HODL X Warriors, the status every HODL Warrior aspires to earn.

Inside Block Topia, Queen Sarah P-fly rules with her Crypto Moongirls army. The engineered Bitcoin X Kids belong to that inside generation; some escape and join Alfie’s Army outside. Birth inside the city, belonging to the outside community and enlistment are different stages, rather than interchangeable labels.

NULL THE PROPHET threatens the meaning of names, evidence and allegiance throughout the HODL WARS. A common enemy does not dissolve the conflict between the underworld and the Queen’s controlled city. Alfie’s own danger is becoming a permanent answer for a movement built around freedom.

Source traditions

Older Bitcoin KiD origin stories, including the SatoRebel contract tradition, remain attributed accounts. They do not settle every version of Alfie’s birth. Earlier portrayals on this site as only a mascot or apprentice have been superseded by his developed role as Army leader. The public Year 3008 story has no settled Final Fork outcome.
