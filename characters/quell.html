Quell

Chain Scribe warrior

Record type: Fictional character.

Read the developed account in Quell, or start with the main Crypto Moonboys story.

In the story

Quell leads Chain Scribes at the First Merge in Brixton in The Calling. This is a warrior and witness role within a particular gathering, rather than a generic embodiment of peace.

Place in the wider world

Leading a response puts practical pressure on the Scribes’ duty to preserve testimony. Records must travel with people through danger; an archive is not protected merely because a keeper survives.

Source traditions

An older peacekeeper portrayal is distinct. Quell has no established universal ceasefire power or permanent command of every Scribe in every period.
