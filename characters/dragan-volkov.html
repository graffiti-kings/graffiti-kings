In the story

Dragan Volkov appears as the fictional Cipher Lord and the extraordinary interview subject in A Satoshi Testament. He gives Ava Chen an account of ledger origins and an object linked to Block Zero.

Place in the wider world

His claimed immortality and origin testimony belong to that story. Ava’s questions make the distinction between a compelling witness and a fully verified record central to the encounter.

Source traditions

The earlier mercenary-captain portrayal is a separate archive tradition. Neither account identifies the real Satoshi Nakamoto or makes Nikola Tesla’s biography part of established Bitcoin history.
