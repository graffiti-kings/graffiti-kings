GraffPUNK cipherkeeper and elder

Record type: Fictional character.

In the story

Rune Tag is a GraffPUNK cipherkeeper and elder associated with vault testimony and countercodes. In the W14 tradition, those codes delay Antichain pressure rather than abolish it.

Place in the wider world

His work makes knowledge useful under strain: a warning must remain interpretable by the people receiving it. A cipherkeeper’s importance does not make every utterance an infallible revelation.

Source traditions

The limits of a countercode matter. A local defence against NULL is not proof of an omnipotent source, a permanent cure or a completed war.
