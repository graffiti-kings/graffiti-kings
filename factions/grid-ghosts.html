Grid Ghosts

W81 Faction 38 — Haunting remnants inside the Eternal Grid

Faction Overview

Grid Ghosts are a Watchers, Prophets and Mystery faction made from residue: erased tags, dead wallets, abandoned game rooms, broken links, failed drops, forgotten forum posts and signals that refuse to disappear. They are not ordinary characters. They are what remains when a mark is removed but the memory of the mark stays active.

Inside W81, Grid Ghosts represent the haunting layer of the universe. Every archive leaves gaps. Every chain contains abandoned addresses. Every wall has paint underneath the new paint. The Ghosts live in those layers.

Role in the Grid

Their role is to remind the factions that deleted things are not always gone. Grid Ghosts appear when history is being rewritten too cleanly, when a faction forgets its root, when a project hides its dead ends, or when the archive needs to recover a missing signal.

They move through broken pathways: missing image slots, corrupted lore fragments, abandoned wallet trails, echoing smart-contract calls and empty districts where the crowd has left but the walls still remember.

Visual Language

Their marks are faded outlines, glitch silhouettes, ghost tags, missing textures, greyed-out crowns, erased names, static and pale paint under a newer layer. A Grid Ghost symbol does not announce itself loudly. It appears when a viewer notices something was meant to be there.

The faction keeps the wiki honest. W81 is not only about victories, crowns and factions; it is also about traces, failures, broken files and memory that survives corruption.

Canon Boundaries

This page treats Grid Ghosts as W81 canon: a mystery faction tied to residue, haunting, deleted marks and broken archive memory. Specific ghosts, powers or events should only be added when supported by the archive or later approved canon.
