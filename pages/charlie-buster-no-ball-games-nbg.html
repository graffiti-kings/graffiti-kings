NO BALL GAMES: British sign-flip culture

NO BALL GAMES is Charlie Buster's creative brand built around a familiar UK-estate restriction sign. The official home and brand history describe taking a prohibitive sign and using it as a symbol of persistence, play and making art anyway. The artist's Grime Art practice supplies the visual language; NO BALL GAMES is a larger brand with physical artworks, clothing, campaigns, digital collecting and fiction.

The five project lanes cover original paintings, hand-painted jackets, creative collaborations, XRP KIDs lore and a collector/community programme. They are published project descriptions; current inventory, commissions and future releases depend on the operating site and direct confirmation.

WAX: the $NBG fan-token plan and the noballgamess NFT collection

A 4 October 2025 whitepaper by Charlie Buster describes $NBG as a WAX-based fan and utility token, naming a maximum supply of 420,000,000 and proposed liquidity, collectible, game, staking and other programmes. A whitepaper proves that a design was published, not that every allocation, pool or promised return was ever deployed. This page does not endorse buying or trading the token.

The maintained Crypto Moonboys NBG wiki explicitly says that $NBG belongs to No Ball Games, not to all forty Crypto Moonboys factions or to GraffPUNKS as a universal currency. For older distribution intentions consult the 10-year decay proposal and the fan-token overview, treating them as historical documents.

The artist's October 2025 WAX onboarding article names the noballgamess NFT collection and links its AtomicHub record. This is distinct from the $NBG fungible token. Marketplace availability and ownership cannot be assumed from old prose; check the collection and current chain transactions directly.

XRPL: No Ball Games XRP KIDs and marketplace records

On the official XRP KIDs hub, the separate XRPL branch is described through a 100-KID genesis creative storyline. The September 2025 XRP KIDs whitepaper gives the project-authored premise; the XRPL.to marketplace collection page is a third-party lookup point for a collection labelled “No Ball Games — By Charlie Buster.”

XRPL.to has displayed a different currently observable supply figure from the story's 100-genesis narrative. Market figures change and may represent only a particular issuer/taxon or trading subset. A published lore count is not proof of exactly 100 outstanding ledger assets. Verify issuer address, taxon, token IDs and latest mint/burn history before making numerical on-chain claims.

The WAX-based $NBG token and the XRPL-based XRP KIDs are different assets on different networks. A similarly named $PMSL story token should not be treated as a launched token unless an independently verifiable issuer and transaction history establish it.

100 / 89 / 11: the fictional future of XRP KIDs

The official fictional lore follows 100 genesis KIDs, 89 taken into the control network of Alpha Aaron and 11 survivors forming FRET (Fresh Rapid Elite Team). It builds through a fictional hard-fork shield called $PMSL, the 1/1 figure Block Buster OG and a New Dawn 2035 future in which the warning signs become gateways.

Important: events labelled 2028, 2029, 2032 and 2035 in the lore are future-dated story chapters, not real-world blockchain trades, arrests, battles, AI deployments, wallet captures or charitable distributions. The fictional “Charlie mind upload” is a narrative device. This wiki separates such stories from real artist records and verified chain transactions.
