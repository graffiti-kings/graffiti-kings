Overview

The Belief Miners are faction 23 in the W81 faction bible. They belong to the Economic and Belief Guardians and are defined by a direct phrase: they do not mine tokens; they mine conviction from new holders and convert it into on-chain power. This makes them one of the clearest bridges between community psychology and blockchain mechanics inside the Graffiti Kings / Crypto Moonboys universe.

They exist because every holder arrives with raw feeling: curiosity, doubt, greed, loyalty, nostalgia, fear, hope or defiance. The Belief Miners refine that unstable energy into something the Grid can use.

Mining Conviction

The word mining is used differently here. Belief Miners are not proof-of-work machines and not token farmers. They extract signal from human commitment. They listen to why a holder joined, what they refuse to sell, what symbol they protect, and which part of the lore made them stay.

Once mined, conviction becomes usable force. It can strengthen a faction, stabilise a vault, power a mural, hold a game economy together or defend a lore archive from being flattened into empty hype. The faction understands that belief is not soft. In W81, belief becomes infrastructure when enough people hold it long enough.

New Holder Transformation

Belief Miners are especially tied to new holders. They stand near the onboarding edge of the Grid, where someone moves from outsider to participant. Their work turns casual attention into faction identity. A user who only holds an asset becomes a holder with a role, then a witness, then a contributor to the shared mythology.

Their visual language should include mining lamps, wallet caves, glowing pickaxes, raw ore shaped like speech bubbles, onboarding tunnels, claim portals and graffiti marks that look like they have been chipped out of concrete rather than sprayed on top of it.

Canon Boundary

This page follows the W81 faction bible. The confirmed canon is that Belief Miners are faction 23, Economic and Belief Guardians, and mine conviction from new holders to convert it into on-chain power. Future expansion should develop rituals, onboarding systems, named miners and faction mechanics without reducing them to ordinary token miners.
