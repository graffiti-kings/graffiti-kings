The real-root name and the Queen character

Sarah PU51FLY is a real-root identity in the Graffiti Kings / Crypto Moonboys project archive. This page keeps that identity separate from Queen Sarah P-fly, the fictional ruler of Block Topia.

The name’s creative connection to a queen character is not permission to invent private biography, employment, family relationships, exhibition credits or future actions. A performer and a role can be connected without being the same kind of record.

Read the fictional Queen on her own page

Queen Sarah P-fly rules Block Topia with the Crypto Moongirls army and its HODL X Warrior order. Some engineered Bitcoin X Kids escape to join Alfie’s Army outside. Those are current story relationships, not real-world activities of Sarah PU51FLY.

The Queen’s authority over the city is distinct from GKniftyHEADS’ authority over the underworld. Facing NULL THE PROPHET does not settle their conflict or reveal the Final Fork’s ending.

The exhibition record is also separate

The real Graffiti Queens exhibition in 2021 and 2022 festival have their own organisers, curator, participating artists and sources. A shared “Queens” name does not establish an individual exhibition credit or make all exhibitors participants in the fiction.

Where this record can grow
