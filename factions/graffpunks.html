GraffPUNKS

GK street faction and plural Paint Path culture

Record type: GK street faction and plural Paint Path culture.

Read the developed account in GraffPUNKS, or start with the main Crypto Moonboys story.

Identity and role

GraffPUNKS carry graffiti rebellion into the Year 3008 struggle over memory, public space and controlled order. Their culture is plural: painters, scouts, cipherkeepers and fighters make different contributions without a single legend owning the entire Paint Path.

Jodie ZOOM 2000 is GraffPUNK-born; Snipey D-Man Sirus scouts; Rune Tag keeps ciphers; Bit-Cap 5000 belongs to an augmented-fighter tradition. Their roles do not turn each character into supreme commander.

Life and practice

Painting is testimony as well as pressure. A mark can preserve a voice excluded from official records, but a rebellion can also turn its own symbols into rules that others must obey. The First Witness keeps that danger visible.

The Burn Crews, Stencil Witches and Tag Lords are GraffPUNK subformations in the older archive. Their names do not automatically create separate political seats in the current Forty.

Command and relationships

GraffPUNKS belong within the GKniftyHEADS-led GK structure. Their members can serve in the united HODL Warriors while remaining GraffPUNKS.

Thera-9’s evidence helps link outside scouts with dissidents inside Block Topia through the Punk Net. The struggle with the Queen’s order continues alongside resistance to NULL.

Sources and boundaries

The real GraffPUNKS broadcast and publishing network is connected by name and culture, not proof that fictional attacks or powers occurred. Older unbuffable-code language is imagery and local story, not a guarantee that any mark or person cannot be erased.
