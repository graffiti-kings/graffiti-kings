Darren Cullen / SER

Graffiti Kings founder and artist · Real-world source record.

Record type: Real creator.

The real artist and the fictional legacy

Darren Cullen, known as SER, is the Graffiti Kings founder and artist documented in this site’s source-attributed timeline. His real work belongs to dated interviews, public art records, exhibition documentation and published credits.

The Crypto Moonboys mythology translates that street-art inheritance into figures called Architect or Forge-Master. Those fictional titles do not replace his biography or prove literal immortality, chain powers or future actions.

Read the dated record

Start with the SER history, press and source network, original commercial website archive and historic production pages. They distinguish retrospective recollections from contemporaneous records and archived business copy.

A real creator can perform a fictional role

Crypto Moonboys is both a creator brand and a cast of fantasy-world characters. Like performers in films, real people can create and inhabit roles while keeping those roles distinguishable from their private lives.

In Year 3008 fiction, GKniftyHEADS leads the underworld structure. Alfie’s Bitcoin Kid Army answers only to that faction; HODL Warriors are the army of all factions’ members. Those commands are world relationships, not statements that a contemporary artist runs an actual army.

Documented public record: beyond the W81 archive

This real-world profile now connects to a separately sourced chronology of Darren Cullen / SER and a press, auction and social-source directory. The sources allow readers to distinguish press coverage, Cullen's own memories and project-authored lore.

In interviews Cullen described the early Croydon writing scene, how SER developed from the South East Rockers name, youth workshops in Mitcham and legal murals at Wallington station. The railway work and its claimed wider impact are recorded as his first-person account, not independently proven project statistics. FAD interview (2012); Page Common interview.

More securely dated external records include the ITV London Tonight cab commission (2012), two Ewbank's auction catalogue lots (2016, both unsold), EXTREME's 2019 production credit and Blockleaders' 2022 WAX feature.

The 2012 arrest and Olympic-venue restrictions were covered by contemporary newspapers; they are reported as allegations and disputed bail conditions, not evidence of guilt. The documented history presents the reporting alongside his own statements without inventing a court outcome. This record remains separate from the fictional role of the SER figure within the Grid.

Graffiti Queens: a documented 2021 exhibition

Contemporary Crypto Briefing reporting identifies Darren Cullen as Graffiti Kings founder and organiser of the April 2021 Graffiti Queens event in Decentraland, curated by Crypto Yuna. Artist Soul'D's first-person account confirms the exhibition was accessible as a virtual skate-park gallery. The dedicated history preserves differing artwork totals and participants' independent accounts, while the WAX registry documents the distinct NFT account system.

Connecting SER's history to Charlie Buster

Charlie Buster's own published biography links his early professional development to Darren Cullen and Graffiti Kings work. The artist history and Saatchi catalogue and web/source directory provide a separate, dated trail for Charlie's art and projects. These are independently identified artwork listings and first-party recollections, not a reason to import unsupported client lists into SER's own biography.

Biography and canon need different evidence

A real career claim needs a public source. A fictional legacy scene needs continuity and source attribution. Neither an old W81 description nor an AI-generated role summary should supply invented employment, family details, awards or project ownership.
