People and Characters

Real creators, performed identities and the cast of the Crypto Moonboys worlds.

People can create and perform characters

Crypto Moonboys is a creator brand and a fantasy-world cast. Real people can inhabit roles as performers bring film characters to life. A fictional action, power or future family relationship is not a biographical fact about the real creator.

This directory separates real-root identities from fictional characters. Each developed character record links to its owning account, with conflicting origins and outcomes kept attributable rather than merged into one unrestricted biography.

Relationships before labels

Alfie commands the market’s Army under orders only from GKniftyHEADS. Queen Sarah commands Block Topia with the Moongirls. Jodie, Aleema and an unnamed third recruit share the Forkborn refusal. Thera-9’s defection supplies another kind of witness. Those relationships are more precise than generic labels such as “mascot” or “system figure.”

GRIT and Grit42 remain distinct. A title such as Princess can have more than one bearer. An alleged origin, a dream account and a local victory remain attached to their source. Read the Concordance before merging them.
