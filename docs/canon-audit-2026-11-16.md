## Editorial decisions

- Apply GK’s current explicit command structure and the reconciled First Witness framework over incompatible old site interpretations.
- Keep Crypto Moonboys as both creator brand and fictional cast; real people may create and perform roles without fictional acts becoming biography.
- GKniftyHEADS + All City Bulls + Nomad Bears + 37 other GK factions = Forty. Alfie leads the market-protection Bitcoin Kid Army under orders only from GKniftyHEADS.
- Bitcoin Kid Army and HODL Warriors are armies, not GK street factions. HODL Warriors combine members of all factions, who retain their identities.
- HODL X Warriors are the distinct Block Topia champion order under Queen Sarah P-fly and her Crypto Moongirls army. All HODL Warriors aspire to that status; aspiration is not completed selection.
- Bitcoin X Kids are the engineered inside generation. Some escape and enlist outside; generation, community and Army are distinct.
- Retain named cultures, subformations, households and older role traditions without inventing extra political seats or forcing a mixed archive list into forty positions. Prior full website wording remains in Git history.
- Read conflicting origins and completed serial outcomes as source-specific witnesses. Keep the public Final Fork unresolved. Preserve the First Witness’s nine Books.
- Preserve the source-attributed art, exhibitions, WAX accounts, press and Wayback records. They are not rewritten as fictional events.
